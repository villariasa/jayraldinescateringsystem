# Beverage Station & Bottomless Drink Logistics Specification

## 1. Equipment Staging
- Twin-bowl acrylic refrigerated beverage dispensers (18L per bowl).
- Heavy-duty acrylic ice buckets with stainless scoops.
- Drip trays with non-slip rubber mats.

## 2. Preparation Ratios
- **House Blend Iced Tea**: 1kg concentrate powder per 10L filtered water + 5kg crushed tube ice.
- **Cucumber Lemonade**: Fresh cucumber pureed extract, lemon cordial, and chilled alkaline water.
