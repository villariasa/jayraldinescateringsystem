# Commissary Cutting Board Color-Coding & Replacement Audit

## 1. Color Standard
- **Red**: Raw beef and pork.
- **Yellow**: Raw poultry.
- **Blue**: Raw seafood and fish.
- **Green**: Washed fruits and salad vegetables.
- **White**: Bakery, cheeses, and ready-to-eat cooked meats.
Boards with deep knife grooves $>2\text{mm}$ are planed or discarded.
