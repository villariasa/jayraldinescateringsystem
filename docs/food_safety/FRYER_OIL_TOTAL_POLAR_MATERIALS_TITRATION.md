# Commercial Deep Fryer Oil Quality & TPM Titration Limits

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Kitchen Safety SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Fry Cooks, Kitchen Leads, QA Auditors

---

## 1. Total Polar Materials (TPM) Measuring
Degraded cooking oil produces harmful oxidation byproducts and unpleasant rancid flavor:
- Test oil daily using a calibrated digital oil tester (Testo 270).
- **Good Condition:** < 18% TPM.
- **Warning Window:** 19% – 23% TPM (skim sediment, filter through 5-micron paper).
- **Mandatory Discard:** **>= 24% TPM** (immediate disposal into waste oil collection drum).

## 2. Daily Filtering SOP
Filter oil at 140°C twice daily through automated filtering pump to remove carbonized batter crumbs.
