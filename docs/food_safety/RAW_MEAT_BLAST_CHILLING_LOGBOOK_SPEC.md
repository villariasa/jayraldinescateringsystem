# Raw Meat Blast Chilling Timelines & Core Probe Logging

## HACCP Standard:
Cooked meats must chill from $60^\circ\text{C}$ to $21^\circ\text{C}$ within 2 hours, and from $21^\circ\text{C}$ to $\le 4^\circ\text{C}$ within an additional 4 hours.
Digital probe timestamps are logged in the commissary tablet QA module.
