# Ready-to-Eat Salad Ambient Dwell Time & Cold Well Policies

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Food Safety Standard  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Banquet Servers, Pantry Chefs

---

## 1. Ambient Temperature Limit
Ready-to-eat (RTE) cold salads (e.g., Potato Salad with mayo, Fresh Garden Greens, Macaroni Salad) are highly vulnerable to bacterial multiplication:
- **Maximum Ambient Exposure:** 2 hours total display time at room temperature (>21°C).
- Display salad bowls on nested crushed ice beds maintaining bowl base <= 4°C.

## 2. Disposal Policy
Any cold salad remaining on buffet tables post-event must be condemned and never recycled into commissary stock.
