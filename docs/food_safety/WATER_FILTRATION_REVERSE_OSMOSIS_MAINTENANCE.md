## 1. Filtration Stages & Replacement Schedule
Commissary ice production and cooking water must pass through a 4-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) filtration skid:

1. **Stage 1 (5-Micron Sediment Filter):** Captures rust and particulates. Replace every 30 days.
2. **Stage 2 (Granular Activated Carbon):** Eliminates municipal chlorine taste and volatile organics. Replace every 60 days.
3. **Stage 3 (Polyamide Composite RO Membrane):** Rejects dissolved solids, heavy metals, and microbes. Replace every 12 months.
4. **Stage 4 (Post-Carbon Polishing & UV Sterilizer):** Inline 254nm ultraviolet lamp. Lamp bulb replacement every 9,000 operational hours.

## 2. Weekly Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) Monitoring
- Tap feed water reference: ~180 to 250 PPM.
- Permeate output standard: strictly **< 20 PPM**.
- If output exceeds 35 PPM, immediate bypass lockdown and membrane flush is mandatory.
