# Raw Poultry Handling & Physical Barrier Cross-Contamination Controls

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Standard Operating Procedure  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Prep Cooks, Stewarding, Commissary Supervisors

---

## 1. Physical Zoning & Color Coding
To eliminate cross-contamination risks associated with *Salmonella enterica* and *Campylobacter jejuni*, poultry processing is restricted to the **Yellow Zone**:

| Equipment / Tool | Color Standard | Sanitization Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| High-Density Polyethylene Cutting Boards | Yellow | Post-batch, max 60 mins |
| Boning & Chef Knives | Yellow Handle / Marked | Post-batch, immediate dip |
| Prep Tables | Stainless Steel Zone Y | Strip wash & chlorine spray |
| Chef Aprons & Nitrile Gloves | Yellow Apron / Blue Gloves | Single use per prep session |

## 2. Prep Workflow Separation
1. **Temporal Separation:** If shared cutting stations are utilized, poultry must be processed after all ready-to-eat salad produce has been sealed, labeled, and refrigerated.
2. **Wash Sink Prohibition:** Raw poultry must never be rinsed in standard prep sinks to avoid aerosolized bacterial splashing up to 1 meter.
3. **Packaging Disposal:** Vacuum plastic wraps and butcher paper must be bagged directly into dedicated bio-hazard lined bins and removed to exterior waste containers immediately.
