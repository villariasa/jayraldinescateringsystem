# Dry Pantry Relative Humidity & Temperature Standards

## Parameters:
- Temperature: $10^\circ\text{C}$ to $21^\circ\text{C}$ ($50^\circ\text{F}$ to $70^\circ\text{F}$).
- Relative Humidity: $\le 50\%$ to prevent caking of spices and flour mold development.
- Shelving: Minimum $15\text{ cm}$ ($6\text{ inches}$) off the floor.
