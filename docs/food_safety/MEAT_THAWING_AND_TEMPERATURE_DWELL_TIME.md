# Raw Meat Thawing Protocols & Temperature Dwell Time Matrix

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Standard Operating Procedure  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Commissary Chefs, Butchery Prep Crew, Quality Assurance

---

## 1. Scope & Critical Control Point (CCP-1)
Thawing bulk proteins (pork shoulder for Humba, beef brisket for Kaldereta, and chicken quarter legs) presents significant microbiological hazard if surface temperatures enter the danger zone (4°C to 60°C / 40°F to 140°F) before internal core defrosting completes.

## 2. Approved Thawing Methodologies

### 2.1 Walk-In Chiller Thawing (Primary Standard)
- **Ambient Target:** 1.5°C to 3.3°C (35°F to 38°F).
- **Placement:** Lowest wire rack shelving inside dedicated drip-retaining food-grade bins. Under no circumstances may raw proteins be stored above produce or pre-cooked stocks.
- **Maximum Dwell Time:**
  - Pork primals (15–20 kg cases): 48 to 72 hours.
  - Poultry (20 kg frozen cartons): 36 to 48 hours.
  - Beef cuts: 48 to 60 hours.

### 2.2 Cold Running Water Rapid Thawing (Emergency Contingency)
- Submerged under continuous potable cold water (<21°C / 70°F).
- Overflow drainage rate must maintain complete water turnover every 15 minutes.
- Maximum duration capped strictly at 120 minutes.

## 3. Digital Temperature Log & Verification
Every thawed batch must have its internal core temperature verified via calibrated bimetallic needle probe and recorded into the commissary ledger prior to marination or butchery portioning.
