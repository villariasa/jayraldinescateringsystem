# Food Contact Surface ATP Bioluminescence Swab Testing Protocol

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** QA Verification Protocol  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Quality Assurance Lead, Sanitation Officers

---

## 1. Principles of ATP Bioluminescent Verification
Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP) bioluminescence provides immediate, objective cleanliness verification by measuring relative light units (RLU) via luciferase reaction.

## 2. Swabbing Protocol & Sampling Points
- **Sample Area:** Standard 10 cm × 10 cm (4" × 4") grid using sterile swab pre-moistened with neutralizing extractant.
- **Rotational Pressure:** Swab while rotating shaft 360 degrees, repeating cross-hatch strokes across surface.

## 3. Threshold Limits & Actions

| Test Point | Pass Threshold (RLU) | Warning Threshold | Failure / Re-clean |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stainless Steel Prep Table | < 30 RLU | 31 – 60 RLU | > 60 RLU (Resanitize immediately) |
| Food Transport Cambro Gasket | < 50 RLU | 51 – 99 RLU | > 100 RLU |
| Banquet Serving Tongs / Ladles | < 20 RLU | 21 – 45 RLU | > 45 RLU |
| Ice Machine Chute Lip | < 25 RLU | 26 – 50 RLU | > 50 RLU |
