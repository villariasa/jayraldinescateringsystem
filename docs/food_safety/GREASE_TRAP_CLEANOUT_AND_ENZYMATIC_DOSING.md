# Commissary Grease Trap Maintenance & Biological Enzymatic Dosing

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Facilities Maintenance SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Maintenance Staff, Stewarding Lead

---

## 1. Cleaning Frequency
- Stainless steel under-sink grease interceptors must be manually scraped and skimmed bi-weekly.
- Central in-ground exterior grease trap pumped monthly by municipal certified environmental service.

## 2. Nightly Automated Enzymatic Dosing
- Deploy peristaltic chemical dosing pump injecting bacterial enzyme consortium (lipase/protease) at 01:00 AM into primary kitchen drains to digest residual animal fats.
