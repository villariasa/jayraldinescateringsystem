# Commissary Fresh Produce Aqueous Ozone Wash Sanitation Cycle

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Sanitization SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Prep Crew, Commissary QA

---

## 1. Ozone Sterilization Principles
Dissolved aqueous ozone (O3) oxidizes bacterial spores and pesticide residues without chemical chlorine aftertaste:
- **Target Concentration:** 1.5 to 2.5 PPM dissolved ozone.
- **Submersion Dwell Time:** Minimum 3 minutes under gentle bubbler agitation.

## 2. Centrifugal Spin Drying
- Post-wash greens transferred directly to commercial spin dryers.
- Spin for 90 seconds to eliminate excess moisture before vacuum bagging for banquet salads.
