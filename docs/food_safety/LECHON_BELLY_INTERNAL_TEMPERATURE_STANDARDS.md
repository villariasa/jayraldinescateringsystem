# Roast Lechon Belly Core Temperature Standards & Crackling Moisture Control

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Culinary QA Standard  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Roasting Chefs, Commissary Supervisors, Banquet Carvers

---

## 1. Cooking Temperature & Pathogen Lethality
- Roll-tied Lechon Belly (pork belly stuffed with lemongrass, garlic, and scallions) must reach a minimum internal core temperature of **74°C (165°F)** held for 15 seconds.
- Verify with calibrated needle probe at geometric center avoiding interior herb stuffing void.

## 2. Crackling Skin Moisture Barrier
- Avoid covering roast lechon belly with tight foil wrap during transit; condensation ruins blistered crisp skin.
- Transport in breathable, vented warm boxes to preserve auditory crunch.
