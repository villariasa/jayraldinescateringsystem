# Food Handler Allergen Segregation & Notification Matrix

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Allergen Management Spec  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Menu Planners, Head Chefs, Catering Coordinators

---

## 1. Philippine Common Catering Allergens
- **Crustaceans / Shellfish:** Crab Relleno, Shrimp Gambas, Halabos na Hipon.
- **Peanuts / Tree Nuts:** Kare-Kare peanut gravy, almond crusted desserts.
- **Soy / Gluten:** Soy sauce (Toyo), oyster sauce marinades, egg noodles (Bam-i, Pancit Guisado).
- **Dairy / Lactose:** Chicken Cordon Bleu cheese core, creamy mushroom sauce.
- **Eggs:** Lumpia Shanghai egg binding, dessert leches and pastries.

## 2. Commissary Kitchen Segregation Rules
- **Dedicated Cookware:** Marked purple silicone handles for designated allergen-sensitive preparations.
- **Deep Frying Isolation:** Shellfish items must never share frying oil with battered chicken or vegetable lumpia.
- **Buffet Labeling:** Every chafing dish placard must explicitly display iconographic allergen warnings for attendee transparency.
