# Chemical Sanitizer Concentration & PPM Titration Guide

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Sanitization Guidelines  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Stewarding Staff, Commissary Supervisors

---

## 1. Approved Sanitizing Agents & Target Concentrations

| Chemical Type | Application Surface | Active PPM Range | Water Temp Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sodium Hypochlorite (Bleach) | Stainless prep tables, dish sanitizing | 100 – 200 PPM | 24°C – 45°C (75°F – 115°F) |
| Quaternary Ammonium (Quat) | Chafing frames, banquet equipment | 200 – 400 PPM | 24°C – 35°C (75°F – 95°F) |
| Peracetic Acid (Organic) | Salad spinner baskets, raw fruit wash | 50 – 80 PPM | 15°C – 25°C (59°F – 77°F) |

## 2. Testing Frequency & Strip Storage
- **Frequency:** Every 2 hours during continuous prep shifts, or upon refreshing wash buckets.
- **Exposure Time:** Dip chemical indicator test paper for 10 seconds; match immediate color change against scale under natural or 5000K daylight LED.
- **Storage:** Test strip vials must remain sealed in humidity-controlled cabinets to prevent atmospheric degradation.
