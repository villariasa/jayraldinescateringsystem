# Commissary Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Weekly Audit Log

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Environmental Safety SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Sanitation Officer, Facilities Manager

---

## 1. Structural Barriers
- Air curtains over all exterior loading bay doors with automatic magnetic door switch triggers.
- Heavy rubber door sweeps with < 3mm threshold gaps to exclude rodents.
- Ultrasonic pest deterrents and sticky trap boards inspected weekly.

## 2. Chemical Treatment Records
- Monthly fumigation carried out strictly by licensed third-party pest control providers outside production shifts.
- 4-hour post-treatment airing and complete food contact surface sanitization before kitchen reopen.
