# Hot Holding Thermistor Probe Calibration Standard Operating Procedure

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Quality Assurance SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Kitchen Leads, Banquet Captains, Maintenance Techs

---

## 1. Overview
All banquet hot-holding units (Cambro insulated warmers, electric steam tables, and field chafing pans) must hold core product temperatures above **60°C (140°F)** throughout 4-hour banquet services. Probes must be calibrated bi-weekly.

## 2. Dual-Point Ice & Boiling Water Calibration

### 2.1 Ice-Water Slurry Method (Low Point: 0.0°C ± 0.5°C)
1. Fill an insulated container with crushed ice to the brim.
2. Add chilled tap water until voids are filled (do not float ice).
3. Insert probe 50 mm into slurry without touching container walls.
4. Stir gently for 60 seconds until reading stabilizes.
5. If reading exceeds ±0.5°C deviation, adjust calibration trimpot or apply software offset in tablet calibration tool.

### 2.2 Boiling Water Method (High Point: 100.0°C at Sea Level)
1. Boil distilled water at a rolling boil.
2. Immerse probe tip 50 mm below meniscus.
3. Verify stabilization at 100.0°C (accounting for barometric altitude offset).
4. Record calibration event into QA Equipment Register.
