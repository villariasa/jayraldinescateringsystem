# Cold Chain Insulated Carrier Monitoring & Thermal Retention Logs

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** HACCP Standard  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Commissary Dispatchers, QA Inspectors, Delivery Drivers

---

## 1. Insulated Food Pan Carrier Performance
- **Equipment:** Cambro Camtainer / Ultra Pan Carriers with polyurethane insulation.
- **Hot Food Retention:** Minimum 65°C after 4 hours transit without supplemental heat.
- **Cold Food Retention:** Maximum 4°C after 4 hours transit using frozen Camchillers.

## 2. Dispatch & Arrival Temperature Verification
- Record food temperature at moment of sealing in commissary.
- Re-probe core temperature immediately upon arrival at event venue.
- Any batch dropping below 60°C hot threshold must undergo immediate induction re-thermalization.
