# Banquet Food Retention Sample Freezing & 72-Hour Audit Chain

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Legal & HACCP SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** QA Officer, Commissary Lead Chef

---

## 1. Sampling Protocol
For every catered event exceeding 50 guests:
- Take 150g sterile retention sample of each served meat, sauce, starch, and dessert directly from the active buffet line.
- Place inside pre-labeled tamper-evident specimen bags with event ID, dish name, and sampling timestamp.

## 2. Retention Freezer Storage
- Store samples in designated -18°C retention freezer for minimum **72 hours**.
- If no adverse health reports are filed by event host within 72 hours, samples are safely autoclaved and discarded.
