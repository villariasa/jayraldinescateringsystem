# Blast Chilling Cycles & Cook-Chill Production Guidelines

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Production Standard  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Executive Chef, Sous Chefs, Commissary Team

---

## 1. The Two-Stage Cooling Mandate
Foods cooked in bulk batches (e.g., Kaldereta braising gravy, Sweet & Sour pork bases, Sotanghon stocks) must transition through temperature stages safely:

- **Stage 1:** 60°C down to 21°C (140°F to 70°F) within **120 minutes (2 hours)**.
- **Stage 2:** 21°C down to 4°C (70°F to 40°F) within **240 minutes (4 hours)**.
- **Total Permissible Window:** Maximum 6 continuous hours.

## 2. Blast Chiller Operation Protocol
1. **Pan Depth Limitations:** Maximum 50 mm (2 inches) food depth in shallow stainless steel gastro-norm pans.
2. **Surface Air Circulation:** Leave 25 mm gap between pan lids or use perforated cooling covers until temperature reaches 4°C.
3. **Core Probe Placement:** Position blast chiller needle probe into the thickest geometric center of the batch.
4. **Automated Alarm:** If target core 4°C is not reached within 90 minutes in blast mode, compressor airflow warning is logged.
