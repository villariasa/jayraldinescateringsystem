# Food Safety Incident Escalation & Batch Recall Action Plan

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Emergency Response Plan  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Management, Legal, Head Chefs, Banquet Operations

---

## 1. Trigger Conditions
An incident response is initiated upon:
- Customer report of suspected gastrointestinal distress.
- Commissary discovery of temperature breach >2 hours during transport.
- Contamination discovery in raw ingredient batch post-dispatch.

## 2. Rapid Response Escalation Ladder
1. **Immediate Quarantine (0 – 15 Mins):**
   - Banquet Captain orders chafing dish removed from buffet line under generic pretext (e.g., dish rotation).
   - Reserve 500g retention sample into sterile tamper-evident bio-bag; flash freeze at -18°C for laboratory culture.
2. **Batch Traceability (15 – 45 Mins):**
   - Commissary lead audits SQLite booking ledger for shared batches delivered to concurrent events.
   - Dispatch hotline contacts parallel Banquet Captains to pull affected dish.
3. **Client Care & Transparency (Within 2 Hours):**
   - Management contacts event host with transparent, professional remediation support.
