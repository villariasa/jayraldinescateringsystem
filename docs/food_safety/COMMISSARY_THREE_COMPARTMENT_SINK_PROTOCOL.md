# Commissary Three-Compartment Manual Warewashing Protocol

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Sanitation SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Dishwashing Stewards, Kitchen Operations

---

## 1. Sink Architecture & Operational Sequence

```
[ Sink 1: WASH ] ───────► [ Sink 2: RINSE ] ───────► [ Sink 3: SANITIZE ] ──────► [ AIR DRY ]
   Pot & Pan Soap             Clean Hot Water           Chemical Quat (200PPM)     Wire Slatted Racks
   Min 45°C (113°F)          Min 43°C (110°F)           Min 24°C, 60s contact      No Towel Drying!
```

## 2. Step-by-Step Execution Rules
1. **Scrape & Pre-Rinse:** Remove solid food debris and grease slurry into designated organic waste bins before items enter Sink 1.
2. **Wash Basin:** Scrub with heavy-duty scouring pads using commercial emulsifying detergent at minimum 45°C.
3. **Rinse Basin:** Clear rinse bath to remove detergent film. Water must be renewed whenever cloudiness occurs.
4. **Sanitize Basin:** Total submersion for minimum 60 continuous seconds in verified 200–400 PPM Quat solution.
5. **Air Drying:** All wares must dry naturally on slanted chrome shelves. Towel wiping is strictly prohibited to prevent cross-contamination.
