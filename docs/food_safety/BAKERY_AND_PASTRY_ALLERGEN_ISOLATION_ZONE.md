# Bakery & Dessert Prep Room Allergen Isolation & Flour Dust Containment

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Facility Engineering Standard  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Pastry Chefs, Commissary Engineers

---

## 1. Physical Room Isolation
- Separate pastry kitchen with dedicated positive air pressure ventilation to prevent airborne wheat flour dust from settling on gluten-free banquet orders.
- Dedicated stainless planetary mixers labeled for exclusive dairy / egg / nut handling.

## 2. Color-Coded Silicone Tools
- Purple spatulas and bowls reserved for allergen-declared bespoke pastry items.
