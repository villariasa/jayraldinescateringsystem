# Raw Seafood Delivery Inspection & Freshness Scoring Matrix

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Commissary Ingress Standard  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Receiving Stewards, Commissary Head Chef

---

## 1. Sensory Quality Attributes
- **Eyes:** Clear, bright, convex; reject sunken or cloudy red corneas.
- **Gills:** Bright red or pink, moist and sweet-smelling; reject brown slime.
- **Flesh:** Firm and elastic to gentle thumb pressure; leaves no permanent indentation.
- **Odor:** Fresh ocean breeze aroma; reject ammonia or sour notes.

## 2. Receiving Temperature
- Delivered packed in crushed slurry ice at core temperature **<= 2°C (36°F)**.
