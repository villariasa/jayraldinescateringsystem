# ReportLab PDF Layout Specification for Official Invoices

## 1. Page Layout Metrics
- **Dimensions**: Standard A4 (`595.27 x 841.89` pt) with $36\text{pt}$ margins.
- **Header Structure**: High-resolution brand logo, business TIN, registered address, and official receipt series number.
- **Table Components**: Alternating zebra row shading, right-aligned currency columns, and payment terms legal footer.
