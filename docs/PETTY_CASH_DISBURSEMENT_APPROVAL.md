# Petty Cash Disbursement & Emergency Procurement Approval

## 1. Approval Workflow
- **Level 1 ($\le ₱2,000$)**: Authorized by on-site Banquet Captain for emergency supplies (ice, extra bottled water, fuel).
- **Level 2 ($> ₱2,000$)**: Requires Owner PIN authorization in desktop settings.
- **Receipt Reconciliation**: Physical receipts submitted within 24 hours for official petty cash reimbursement.
