# Data Privacy Compliance (RA 10173 / NPC Guidelines)

## 1. Compliance Controls
Implements privacy safeguards mandated by the National Privacy Commission (NPC):

- **Data Minimization**: Collects only contact, venue, and billing info required for event fulfillment.
- **Restricted Access**: Administrative screens require owner authentication bypass codes.
- **Audit Logging**: Logs all customer record edits, exports, and profile access with timestamped device IDs.
- **Data Retention**: Automatically archives completed booking records older than 5 years to encrypted backup storage.
