# Halal Food Preparation & Segregated Storage Specification

## 1. Procurement Verification
All halal-designated meats must be sourced from suppliers accredited by the National Commission on Muslim Filipinos (NCMF).

## 2. Segregation Protocols
- Dedicated green-handled chef knives and color-coded cutting boards.
- Completely isolated prep tables and separate cooking woks.
- Halal food warmers labeled with distinct identification tags on the buffet line.
