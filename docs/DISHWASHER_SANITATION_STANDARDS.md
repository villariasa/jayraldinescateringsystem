# Commercial Warewashing Sanitation & Flatware Sterilization Protocol

## 1. Sanitization Cycles
- **Wash Cycle**: 65°C (150°F) with low-foam commercial alkaline detergent.
- **Final Sanitizing Rinse**: 82°C (180°F) for minimum 15 seconds to ensure bacterial eradication.

## 2. Flatware Handling
- Silverware is sorted into perforated cylinders, washed standing upright, and UV-cabinet dried before packaging in sterile cloth rolls.
