# Leftover Food Packaging & Liability Waiver Policy

## 1. Packaging Protocol
- Upon buffet closing (T + 2.5 hours), remaining buffet food is transferred into client-provided or catering-supplied microwaveable containers.
- Hot food must be cooled quickly or stored in insulated takeaway boxes.

## 2. Food Safety Release Form
The booking contract explicitly states that Jayraldine's Catering assumes no liability for food consumed 2 hours post-event egress.
