## 1. Operational Protocol

A seamless catering operation depends on strict adherence to venue ingress (setup) and egress (teardown) timelines.

### 1.1 Ingress Timeline (T-minus 3 Hours to Event Call Time)

- **T-180m:** Arrival at venue loading bay; check-in with venue security and building management.
- **T-150m:** Unloading of catering trucks: buffet tables, chafing dishes, dining chairs, linen sets, and beverage bar equipment.
- **T-120m:** Layout and skirting of banquet tables according to client-approved floor plan.
- **T-90m:** Chafing dish water pans filled and Sterno canned heat ignited for pre-heating.
- **T-60m:** Insulated food warmers (Cambro carriers) staged in prep area.
- **T-30m:** Food items transferred to buffet chafers; garnishes and temperature verification logged.
- **T-00m:** Event starts; captain waiter greets host and commences service.

---

## 2. Egress Timeline (Post-Event Teardown)

- **T+00m:** Service concludes; captain secures host signature on Event Completion Slip.
- **T+15m:** Leftover food packed into food-grade containers and turned over to host.
- **T+30m:** Chafers cooled, emptied, and loaded into transport crates.
- **T+60m:** Dining area swept, garbage bagged in heavy-duty sacks, venue turnover completed.
