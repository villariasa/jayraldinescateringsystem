# Catering Equipment Breakage & Security Deposit Reconciliation Policy

> **Target Version:** v4.2.3  
> **Status:** Approved / Active Specification  
> **Date:** October 6, 2026  
> **Component:** Operations, Billing Settlement, Inventory Audit

---

## 1. Overview

For off-premise banquets, weddings, and party equipment rentals, a refundable security deposit is collected to cover broken glassware, damaged linens, or missing chaffing utensils.

---

## 2. Standard Replacement Fee Schedule

| Equipment Item | Unit Replacement Cost (PHP) |

---

## 3. Reconciliation Workflow

1. **Captain's Post-Event Tally:** Waitstaff counts all returning crates and logs discrepancies on the mobile tablet app.
2. **Client Sign-off:** The event host reviews the breakage list with the captain.
3. **Automatic Deductions:** In the settlement dialog, breakage fees are automatically deducted from the Security Deposit. Remaining deposit balance is returned via Cash or GCash.
