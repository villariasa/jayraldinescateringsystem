# Catering Equipment Maintenance & Burner Safety Runbook

## 1. Chafing Dish Care
- **Water Pan Maintenance**: Fill water pan with 1 inch of hot water before lighting fuel. Never light chafers dry.
- **Descaling Protocol**: Soak pans weekly in mild food-grade citric acid solution to dissolve mineral water scale.
- **Polishing**: Use non-abrasive microfiber cloths and stainless steel polish on exterior roll-top lids.

## 2. Chafing Fuel Canister Safety
- Inspect wick integrity and shelf-life date stamps prior to dispatch.
- Keep flame height regulated; never stack burning canisters.
