# Cold Chain Transport & Refrigeration Monitoring

## 1. Temperature Assurance Standards
- **Chilled Transit**: Perishable salads and dessert cakes transported in insulated thermo-coolers with reusable gel ice packs.
- **Meat Storage**: Raw meats held at $0^{\circ}\text{C} - 2^{\circ}\text{C}$ in dedicated walk-in chillers prior to cooking.
- **Temperature Checks**: Verified via digital infrared thermometers upon loading and venue arrival.
