# Vendor Reliability KPI Scorecard & Quality Assessment

## 1. Scorecard Metrics
- **On-Time Delivery Rate** ($40\%$ weight): Delivery within agreed morning commissary prep windows.
- **Freshness & Grade Quality** ($40\%$ weight): Zero rejection of meat marbling, poultry temperature, and vegetable crispness.
- **Price Stability** ($20\%$ weight): Adherence to contracted 90-day wholesale price lock agreements.
