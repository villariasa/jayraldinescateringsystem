# Banquet Staff Service Etiquette & VIP Customer Care

## 1. Service Standards
- **Guest Greeting**: Warm, professional greeting with right-hand chest placement.
- **Buffet Attendant Role**: Assist guests with plate handling, announce dish names, and maintain spotless chafing stations.
- **Complaint De-escalation**: Listen attentively, apologize sincerely, replace dish immediately, and notify banquet captain.
