# Lechon de Cebu Procurement & Live Carving Protocols

## 1. Roasting & Carving Timeline
- **Pig Sourcing**: $35\text{kg} - 45\text{kg}$ live weight native pigs sourced from local Cebu livestock farms.
- **Roasting Duration**: $3.5\text{ to }4.0\text{ hours}$ over coconut shell charcoal coals with continuous lemongrass basting.
- **Carving Station Setup**: Stainless steel carving board, warming lamps, native spicy vinegar dipping sauces, and fresh liver gravy.
