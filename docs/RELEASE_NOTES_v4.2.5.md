## Highlights of Version 4.2.5
Version 4.2.5 introduces a landmark 60-part operational architecture, food safety, banquet staffing, field logistics, contract governance, and tablet UX specification suite.

### 1. Food Safety & HACCP Standards
- Standardized meat thawing dwell times and raw poultry physical barrier zoning.
- Dual-point ice/boiling calibration for hot-holding thermistor temperature probes.
- Chemical sanitizer titration procedures and three-compartment sink warewashing protocols.

### 2. Banquet Staffing & Labor Compliance
- Formulated guest-to-server staffing ratios for standard, assisted, and plated dining.
- Codified Philippine Labor Code 1.25x overtime and 10% night differential payroll calculations.
- Published uniform grooming checklists and banquet captain pre-service briefing standards.

### 3. Field Logistics & Electrical Infrastructure
- Designed double-sided mirrored buffet line traffic flow layouts to eliminate queuing bottlenecks.
- Engineered 10 kW generator load balancing for high-amperage holding warmers and live carveries.
- Released outdoor gazebo wind threshold guidelines and anemometer safety triggers.

### 4. Client Relations & Contractual Safeguards
- Created sensory scoring worksheets for prospective client menu tastings.
- Established strict 7-day headcount freeze rules and force majeure weather clauses.
- Standardized outside beverage corkage schedules and broken centerpiece replacement billing.

### 5. Tablet PWA & Mobile Ergonomics
- Enforced 48px minimum touch target dimensions for fast-paced service interactions.
- Designed outdoor sunlight high-contrast mode with automatic ambient lux triggering.
- Defined ESC/POS thermal receipt formatting for 58mm mobile Bluetooth printers.
- Specified kitchen bump bar keypad interfaces and multi-tablet lease collision prevention.
