# Environmental Sustainability & Waste Segregation Policy

## 1. Waste Sorting
Kitchen stewards maintain three distinct color-coded waste bins at every banquet venue:
- **Green**: Organic food scraps for agricultural composting.
- **Blue**: Recyclable plastic water bottles, tin cans, and cardboard packaging.
- **Black**: Non-recyclable sanitary refuse.

## 2. Zero Single-Use Plastics
Straws, beverage cups, and cocktail picks must be 100% paper or bamboo biodegradable.
