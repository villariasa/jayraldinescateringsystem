# Philippine Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Tax Calculation Rules

## 1. Tax Computation Formulas
For VAT-registered catering transactions:

$$\text{VATable Sales} = \frac{\text{Gross Amount}}{1.12}$$

$$\text{Value Added Tax (12\%)} = \text{Gross Amount} - \text{VATable Sales}$$

$$\text{Creditable Withholding Tax (2\%)} = \text{VATable Sales} \times 0.02$$
