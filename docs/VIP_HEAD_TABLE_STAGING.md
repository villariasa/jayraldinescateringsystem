# VIP Presidential Head Table Setup & Silver Service Standards

## 1. Table Topography
- Elevated riser platform with custom-pleated backdrop.
- Individual charger plates, lead crystal water goblets, and folded linen napkins.
- Low-profile floral centerpieces preventing sightline obstruction between guests and photographers.

## 2. Plated Presidential Service
VIP guests are served simultaneously by trained silver-service attendants entering from the guest's right side.
