# Booking Deposit Schedules, Payment Tranches & Refund Penalties

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Financial Contract Spec  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Billing Department, Event Coordinators, Clients

---

## 1. Payment Tranche Schedule
- **Tranche 1 (Reservation Deposit):** ₱10,000 to secure date and booking lock.
- **Tranche 2 (Mid-Term Payment):** 50% of total contract due 30 days prior to event.
- **Tranche 3 (Final Settlement):** Full remaining balance cleared minimum 3 business days prior to dispatch.

## 2. Cancellation Penalty Matrix
- > 60 Days: Full refund minus ₱3,000 administrative processing fee.
- 30 to 59 Days: Forfeiture of ₱10,000 reservation deposit.
- < 14 Days: 50% contract forfeiture to cover dedicated food procurement.
