# Catering Contract Headcount Lock & Final Adjustment Window

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Legal & Commercial Policy  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Sales Coordinators, Clients, Accounting

---

## 1. The 7-Day Lock Rule
- **T - 14 Days:** Preliminary headcount review; menu changes finalized.
- **T - 7 Days:** Final guaranteed headcount strictly locked.
- Headcount may increase up to 10% subject to kitchen ingredient availability, but may **never decrease** after T - 7 days due to bulk commissary procurement.

## 2. Buffer Food Production
Jayraldine's automatically prepares a **5% complimentary buffer** above guaranteed headcount to accommodate unexpected guest arrivals without running short.
