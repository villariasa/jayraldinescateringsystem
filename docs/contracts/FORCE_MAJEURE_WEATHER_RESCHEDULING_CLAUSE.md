# Force Majeure Protocols & Severe Weather Rescheduling Terms

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Legal & Contract Terms  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Legal, Managing Director, Sales Staff

---

## 1. Definition of Qualifying Force Majeure Events
- PAGASA Tropical Cyclone Wind Signal (TCWS) No. 2 or higher declared over Metro Cebu.
- Civil unrest, road blockades, or government-mandated pandemic movement restrictions.
- Venue structural failure or flash flooding rendering access impossible.

## 2. Rescheduling & Deposit Retention Rules
1. **Zero Forfeiture:** The client's paid reservation downpayment is preserved in full and credited toward a rescheduled date within **180 calendar days**.
2. **Perishable Recovery Fee:** If cancellation occurs <24 hours before the event after meat butchery and cooking have occurred, actual raw ingredient costs (up to 30% of contract total) are deducted from credit.
