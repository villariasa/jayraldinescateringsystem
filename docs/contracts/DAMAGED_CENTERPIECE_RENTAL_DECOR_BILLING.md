## 1. Standard Replacement Fee Schedule

|---|---|---|

## 2. Security Deposit Deduction Flow
Broken or missing items documented on the post-event inventory sheet are deducted directly from the client's **₱3,000.00 incidental security deposit**. Remaining balances are refunded via GCash within 48 hours.
