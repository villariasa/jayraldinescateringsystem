# Final Payment Reminders & Billing Dispute Escalation Flow

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Accounting SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Billing Department, Accounting Staff

---

## 1. Automated Reminder Schedule
The desktop billing engine sends milestone payment prompts:
- **T-30 Days:** Friendly reminder of remaining balance and payment channels.
- **T-7 Days:** Final balance demand notice (GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
- **Day of Event:** Downpayment must be at minimum 50% (or 100% for non-corporate clients) prior to food dispatch.

## 2. Dispute Resolution Protocol
If a client disputes additional headcounts or extra beverage charges after the event:
1. Captain provides the signed **On-Site Guest Count Verification Slip**.
2. If discrepancy remains unresolved, Billing Manager is authorized to adjust within 5% discretionary tolerance; balances >₱5,000 require Managing Director sign-off.
