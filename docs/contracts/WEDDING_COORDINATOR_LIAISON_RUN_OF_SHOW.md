# Wedding Coordinator Liaison Protocol & Run-of-Show Synchronization

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Operations Protocol  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Banquet Captain, Wedding Planners

---

## 1. Coordination Milestones
The Banquet Captain coordinates directly with the couple's wedding planner:
- **T-120 Mins:** Confirm banquet table layout matches planner's seat plan.
- **T-30 Mins:** Verify cake table, champagne glasses, and sound system audio feed for speeches.
- **T-0 Mins (Reception Start):** Sync timing for bridal march, first dance, and prayer before opening buffet.

## 2. Program Flexibility Buffer
Catering teams maintain food holding buffers up to **45 minutes** in Cambro warmers to accommodate delayed church ceremonies or extended photo sessions without food quality loss.
