# VIP Dietary Exception (Vegan, Gluten-Free, Halal) Handling Protocol

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Service Protocol  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Banquet Captains, Special Diets Cook, Sales

---

## 1. Advance Registration (Minimum 10 Days)
All VIP guest dietary requests (Vegetarian, Vegan, Celiac Gluten-Free, Halal dietary respect) must be registered in the booking record detail notes:
- Record guest name, allocated table number, and exact restriction.

## 2. Kitchen Preparation & Service Delivery
1. Special meals are packaged in sealed, labeled individual heat containers at the commissary.
2. Labeled containers are regenerated in clean, unshared microwave warmers or separate water baths.
3. Served directly to the guest by the Banquet Captain by name prior to opening general buffet lines.
