## 1. Corkage Fee Matrix

| Outside Item Category | Corkage Assessment | Included Service Amenities |
|---|---|---|
| Wine & Champagne (Standard 750ml) | ₱250.00 / bottle | Wine bucket, ice, glassware, service uncorking |
| Hard Liquor / Spirits (1 Liter) | ₱500.00 / bottle | Highball glassware, ice refills, bar service |
| Whole Roast Lechon (Third-Party) | ₱1,000.00 / lechon | Dedicated carving table, knife service, warming lamp |
| Wedding Cake (Commercial Baker) | ₱0.00 (Waived) | Cake table, linen, cake knife, dessert plating |

## 2. Food Safety Waiver Requirement
Clients bringing outside whole roast lechon must execute a **Third-Party Food Indemnity Waiver** releasing Jayraldine's Catering from foodborne illness claims arising from non-commissary items.
