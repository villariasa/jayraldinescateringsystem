# Post-Event Client Feedback Collection & NPS Scoring Protocol

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Customer Success SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Sales Support, Customer Relations

---

## 1. Survey Dispatch Timing (T+24 Hours)
Within 24 hours of event conclusion, an automated feedback link is sent via SMS and email:
- "On a scale of 0 to 10, how likely are you to recommend Jayraldine's Catering to friends and colleagues?"

## 2. NPS Metric Breakdown
- **Promoters (Score 9–10):** Automatically prompted with Google Maps review invite.
- **Passives (Score 7–8):** Thanked for feedback; logged for internal service analysis.
- **Detractors (Score 0–6):** Immediate phone follow-up within 4 hours by the Operations Manager to investigate issues and offer compensatory dining vouchers.
