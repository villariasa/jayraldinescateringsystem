# Outside Beverage & Food Corkage Fee Schedule and Waivers

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Commercial Policy  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Sales Agents, Event Hosts, Bar Managers

---

## 1. Standard Corkage Rates
- **Hard Liquor / Wine:** ₱300 per 750ml bottle (covers glassware par, chilling ice, and steward washing).
- **Whole Roast Pig (Outside Lechon):** ₱1,500 handling fee (covers live carver, chopping block, warming trays, and vinegar dipping sauce).
- **Dessert / Grazing Station:** ₱2,000 utility fee if utilizing Jayraldine's display tables and glassware.

## 2. Promotional Waivers
Waivers may be authorized by Senior Sales Manager when client books tier packages exceeding 150 pax.
