# Guest Headcount Adjustments & 7-Day Order Freeze Policy

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Contract Policy  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Sales Coordinators, Billing Admin, Clients

---

## 1. Headcount Adjustment Thresholds
- **Upward Revisions (+Guests):** Permitted up to 7 calendar days before the event, subject to commissary ingredient procurement availability.
- **Downward Revisions (-Guests):** Allowed up to 14 days before the event, not exceeding 10% of initial booked pax.

## 2. The 7-Day Final Order Freeze
At **T minus 7 calendar days**:
1. All menu selections, dish customizations, and headcount numbers are locked in the SQLite booking record.
2. Commissary generates bulk supplier purchase orders based on frozen numbers.
3. Any last-minute additions within 7 days incur a **15% rush procurement surcharge** per additional guest.
