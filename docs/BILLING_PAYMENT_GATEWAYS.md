# Billing, Split Payments & Digital Wallet Integration Guide

## 1. Payment Lifecycle
1. **Reservation Deposit**: Standard 30% advance booking fee.
2. **Progress Milestone**: 50% midpoint payment 14 days prior to event.
3. **Final Settlement**: Remaining 20% balance + on-the-day incidentals paid upon event conclusion.

## 2. Payment Methods Supported
- Cash payment with automated change calculation and cash drawer trigger.
- GCash & Maya dynamic QR code generation.
- Bank wire transfers (BDO, BPI) with reference number tracking.
- Corporate checks with clearance status workflows.
