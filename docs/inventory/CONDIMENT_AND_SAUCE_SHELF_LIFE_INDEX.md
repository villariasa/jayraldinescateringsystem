## 1. Storage & Shelf Life Index

| Prepared Sauce | Storage Method | Max Shelf Life |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet & Sour Sauce | Refrigerator (4°C) | 7 Days |
| Mushroom Gravy | Refrigerator (4°C) | 3 Days |
| Tartar Sauce (Mayo-based) | Refrigerator (2°C) | 48 Hours |
| Caesar Salad Dressing | Refrigerator (2°C) | 72 Hours |
| Homemade Chili Garlic Sauce | Room Temp in sealed jar | 30 Days |\n
