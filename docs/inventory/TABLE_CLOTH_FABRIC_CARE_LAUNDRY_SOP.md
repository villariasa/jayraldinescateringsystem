## 1. Stain Removal Procedures

- **Red Wine / Sauce Stains:** Treat immediately with cold water and enzyme stain remover within 2 hours of return.
- **Wax Drips from Table Centerpieces:** Scrape gently with dull knife; place between paper towels and iron on medium heat to absorb wax.
- **Drying & Pressing:** Steam press round banquet tablecloths while slightly damp to guarantee crease-free table presentations.\n
