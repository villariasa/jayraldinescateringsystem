# Catering Equipment Tracking & Damage Fee Specification

## 1. Tracked Assets
- Chafing Dishes, Burners, and Serving Spoons.
- Glassware, Porcelain Plates, and Stainless Cutlery.
- Table Linens, Seat Covers, and Canopy Tents.

## 2. Settlement Workflow
Equipment shortages returned after banquet service are logged with unit replacement fees added to the client invoice.
