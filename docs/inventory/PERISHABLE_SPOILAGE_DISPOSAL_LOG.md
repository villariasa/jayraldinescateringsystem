# Perishable Inventory Spoilage & Wastage Disposal Log

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active SOP  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Commissary Kitchen, Food Waste Management, Cost Accounting

---

## 1. Purpose
Raw meats, fresh produce, and dairy products that spoil due to equipment failure or exceed shelf life must be documented for food cost variance analysis.

---

## 2. Logging Schema
- Date & Time of Disposal
- Ingredient Name & SKU
- Quantity Discarded (kg / liters)
- Estimated Cost (PHP)
- Root Cause: (e.g. Chiller Breakdown, Expiration, Preparation Error)
- Kitchen Head Signature\n
