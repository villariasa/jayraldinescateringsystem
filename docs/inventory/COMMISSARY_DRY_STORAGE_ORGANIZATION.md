> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Warehouse, Sanitation, FIFO Control

---

## 1. Storage Standards
- **Off-the-Floor Rule:** All dry goods, rice sacks, and flour stored at least **15 cm (6 inches)** off the floor on heavy-duty plastic pallets.
- **Wall Clearance:** Pallets positioned 10 cm away from walls to facilitate airflow and pest inspection.
- **FIFO Tagging:** First-In, First-Out colored sticker system (Red = Use First, Yellow = New Batch).\n
