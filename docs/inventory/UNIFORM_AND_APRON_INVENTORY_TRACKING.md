# Banquet Staff Uniform & Apron Inventory Tracking System

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active Specification  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Staff Assets, Laundry Control, Inventory

---

## 1. Asset Registry
Every server vest, bowtie, chef coat, and black apron is barcoded or RFID tagged to track issue and return cycles.

---

## 2. Loss & Damage Deduction
Staff who fail to return issued uniform pieces at the end of the event shift are assessed standard payroll deductions:
- Server Vest: PHP 400.00
- Chef Coat: PHP 650.00
- Apron: PHP 150.00\n
