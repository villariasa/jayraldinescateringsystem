## 1. Maintenance Intervals

- **Daily:** Chiller & freezer temperature validation, grease trap skimming.
- **Weekly:** Commercial gas range burner descaling, exhaust hood filter degreasing.
- **Monthly:** Walk-in cold storage condenser coil vacuuming, knife sharpening.
- **Quarterly:** Fire suppression system nozzle inspection by BFP licensed contractor.\n
