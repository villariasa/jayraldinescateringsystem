## 1. Cleaning & Polishing Protocol
1. Wash food pans with non-abrasive detergent to prevent scratch marks.
2. Descale water pans weekly using food-grade white vinegar solution to eliminate mineral calcium deposits.
3. Polish outer dome covers with stainless steel cream polish and microfiber cloths before each VIP event.\n
