# Event Venue Power Outage Contingency Standard Operating Procedure

## 1. Contingency Action Plan
- **Phase 1 (Instant)**: Chafing dish fuel burners maintain food hot holding indefinitely without electrical power.
- **Phase 2 (T + 1 min)**: Tablet kiosk automatically operates on internal battery; transactions queue in local SQLite offline journal.
- **Phase 3 (T + 5 min)**: Deploy portable silent inverter generator for PA sound system and spotlighting.
