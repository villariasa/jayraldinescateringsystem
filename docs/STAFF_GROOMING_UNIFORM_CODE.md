# Banquet Service Uniform Code & Hygiene Inspection Policy

## 1. Uniform Specifications
- **FOH Waitstaff**: Crisp black collared polo with embroidered gold company insignia, black trousers, black leather shoes.
- **BOH Chefs & Stewards**: White double-breasted chef coats, black chef aprons, and breathable skull caps.

## 2. Pre-Shift Inspection
The Banquet Captain conducts mandatory checks of clean fingernails, tied hair, polished shoes, and breath mint compliance.
