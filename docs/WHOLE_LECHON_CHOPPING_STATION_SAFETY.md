# Lechon de Cebu Live Chopping Station Safety Protocols

## 1. Live Carving Safety
- **Cut-Resistant Chainmail Gloves**: Stainless steel mesh gloves worn on non-cutting hand during whole pig portioning.
- **Heavy End-Grain Boards**: $4\text{ inch}$ thick solid cutting boards to prevent sliding during cleaver strikes.
- **Splatter Shields**: Clear acrylic sneeze guards protecting guests from hot grease splatters during carving.
