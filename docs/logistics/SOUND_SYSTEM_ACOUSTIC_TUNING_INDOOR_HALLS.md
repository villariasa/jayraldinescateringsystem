# Indoor Event Hall Acoustic Tuning & Audio Feedback Prevention

> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** AV Leads, Sound Technicians

---

## 1. Room Reflection Management
High-ceiling ballroom and concrete social halls create muddy acoustic reverberations:
- Apply high-pass filter (HPF) at 80Hz on all speech vocal channels to eliminate low-end boom.
- Cut problematic room resonant frequencies (typically 250Hz - 400Hz and 2.5kHz - 4kHz) using 31-band graphic equalizer.

## 2. Wireless Mic Gain Staging
- Set transmitter receiver gain so loud vocal speech peaks at -6 dBVU, avoiding clipping distortion on wedding toasts.
