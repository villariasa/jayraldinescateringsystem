## 1. Kitchen Power Budget (Remote Venue Setup)
Remote outdoor locations without municipal utility connections require careful wattage accounting:

| Equipment Item | Rated Wattage | Amperage (220V) | Surge Factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial Holding Warmer (Cambro) | 1,800 W | 8.2 A | 1.2x |
| Coffee Percolator (100-cup) | 1,500 W | 6.8 A | 1.0x |
| Induction Cooktop (Live Station) | 3,500 W | 15.9 A | 1.1x |
| Sound System & LED Par Lights | 1,200 W | 5.5 A | 1.5x |
| **Total Simultaneous Peak** | **8,000 W** | **36.4 A** | — |

## 2. Generator Sizing & Phase Balancing
- Minimum recommended unit: **12 kVA (10 kW continuous) Silent Diesel Generator**.
- Never exceed 80% continuous rated load capacity.
- Keep kitchen equipment and delicate audio/video gear on separate sub-breakers to eliminate electrical feedback.
