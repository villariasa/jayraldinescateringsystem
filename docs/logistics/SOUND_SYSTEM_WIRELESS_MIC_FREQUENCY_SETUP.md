# Portable Sound System Wireless Microphone Frequency Management

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Audio Tech Guide  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** AV Technician, Banquet Captain

---

## 1. UHF Frequency Band Allocation
To avoid interference from local commercial radio and cellular bands in event halls:
- Operational window: **UHF 512 MHz to 542 MHz** or **614 MHz to 698 MHz**.
- Always run automated frequency scan upon arriving at venue before wedding program starts.

## 2. Feedback Suppression & Speaker Positioning
- **Speaker Placement:** Position powered PA speakers forward of the podium and host microphones.
- **Angle:** 15-degree toe-in aimed at center guest seating, away from reflective glass walls.
- **Battery Rule:** Microphones must be equipped with fresh, tested alkaline batteries (>1.45V) before every reception.
