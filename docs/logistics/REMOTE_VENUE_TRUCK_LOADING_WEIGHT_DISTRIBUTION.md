# Logistics Truck Cargo Weight Distribution & Ramp Safety

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Fleet Logistics SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Drivers, Logistics Crew, Loading Supervisors

---

## 1. Axle Weight Distribution Rules
- Place heaviest road cases (chafing sets, gas burners, full beverage crates) centered over or immediately forward of the rear axle.
- Never stack glass crates above shoulder height (1.4m) inside cargo box.
- Secure all mobile racks with heavy nylon ratchet load straps anchored to wall E-tracks.

## 2. Ramp Angle & Wet Weather Safety
- Maximum ramp gradient: 25 degrees.
- Apply non-slip grip tape to aluminum loading ramps; deploy safety jacks under truck tailgates before rolling heavy flight cases.
