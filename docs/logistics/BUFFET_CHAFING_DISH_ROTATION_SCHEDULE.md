# Buffet Chafing Dish Rotation & Replenishment Schedule

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Food Service Standard  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Buffet Captains, Kitchen Runners, Service Staff

---

## 1. Replenishment Trigger Thresholds
- **25% Remaining Capacity:** Food runner immediately alerts back-of-house staging chef.
- **Hot Swap Protocol:** Never pour fresh hot food directly on top of old, cold food in front of guests.
- Always swap the entire gastro-norm insert pan with a fresh, steaming pan from holding Cambros.

## 2. Visual Quality Standards
- Clean rim of chafing frames with dry microfiber cloth between pan swaps.
- Rotate serving tongs and ladles every 45 minutes to prevent sauce crusting.
