# Banquet Chair Stacking Limits & Transport Dolly Handling Safety

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Material Handling SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Warehouse Handlers, Truck Loading Crew

---

## 1. Stacking Thresholds by Chair Architecture

| Chair Model | Maximum Vertical Stack | Storage Footprint |
|---|---|---|
| Tiffany / Chiavari (Resin/Wood) | 7 chairs | Interlocking base |
| Padded Banquet Conference Chair | 10 chairs | Slanted rear spine |
| Plastic Armless Monobloc | 15 chairs | Nested center |

## 2. Dolly Transport Rules
- Always use a customized two-wheel chair hand-truck with angled rear cradle.
- Secure stacks exceeding 8 units with a quick-release bungee strap before traversing truck loading ramps.
- Never tilt stacks past a 30-degree incline while navigating elevators or doorway thresholds.
