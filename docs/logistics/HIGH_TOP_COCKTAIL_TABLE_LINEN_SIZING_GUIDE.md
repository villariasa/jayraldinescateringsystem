## 2. Outdoor Wind Mitigation
For open-air lawn or beachfront banquets:
- Secure loose linen puddles with weighted silicone bands or coordinated ribbon ties.
- Insert hidden 200g stainless drapery weights into interior hem corners to resist gusts up to 25 knots.
