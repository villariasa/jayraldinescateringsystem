# Post-Event Hall Teardown & Venue Sweep Standard Operating Procedure

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Teardown SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Banquet Captain, Closing Crew

---

## 1. Systematic Teardown Sequence
1. **Food Recovery (T+0):** Clear leftover dishes into client-provided take-out containers or seal for commissary return.
2. **Glassware & China (T+15m):** Clear guest tables, separate into padded crates to avoid transit chipping.
3. **Linens (T+30m):** Shake out debris, sort into damp and dry laundry sacks. Never roll wet linens with food grease.
4. **Buffet & Decor (T+45m):** Extinguish and pack chafing gear; disassemble centerpieces.
5. **Trash Sweep & Floor Care (T+60m):** Sweep hall floor, bag all trash, dispose in venue dumpster.

## 2. Venue Sign-Off Clearance
The Banquet Captain must complete the joint walkthrough with venue facility security, obtaining a signed **Venue Clearance Release Slip** certifying zero property damage.
