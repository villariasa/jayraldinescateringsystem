# Post-Event Teardown Equipment Reconciliation & Breakage Audit

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Asset Protection SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Warehouse Inventory Leads, Banquet Captains

---

## 1. Two-Way Equipment Dispatch Manifest
Every outbound truck carries an audited dispatch slip listing:
- Chafing dishes (frames, water pans, food inserts, roll lids).
- Glassware racks (categorized by goblet, flute, highball).
- Cutlery tubs (spoons, forks, knives by weight/count).

## 2. On-Site Return Scan
- Captain and warehouse receiver sign joint inspection sheet at commissary gate.
- Document chipped china, cracked glassware, and missing ladle counts for client deposit adjustment.
