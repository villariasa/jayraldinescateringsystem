# Ice Carving Centerpiece Drainage & Submersible LED Illumination

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Decorative Logistics Spec  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Decor Team, Banquet Captains, Electricians

---

## 1. Meltwater Drainage Basin
- Large ornamental ice sculptures (Swan, Wedding Monograms) generate 4 to 8 liters of meltwater over a 4-hour event.
- Use heavy-duty clear polycarbonate drip trays equipped with flexible silicone siphon hose routed to a concealed 20L bucket under table skirting.

## 2. Submersible Lighting
- Use IP68 waterproof battery-powered RGB puck LEDs placed directly beneath acrylic riser blocks.
- Set to warm amber or cool white matching event theme; avoid rapid color flashing during dinner service.
