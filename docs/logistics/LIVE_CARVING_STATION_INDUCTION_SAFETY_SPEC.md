# Live Carving Station Induction Burner & Sneeze Guard Safety Spec

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Engineering & Safety Spec  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Executive Chef, Banquet Captains, Tech Logistics

---

## 1. Equipment Standards
Live stations (Roast Beef Carvery, Lechon Belly Station) require:
- **Commercial Induction Cooktop:** 3500W rated with ceramic glass surface for gravy holding.
- **Acrylic Sneeze Guard:** Food-grade polycarbonate canopy at 350mm height, providing full barrier between guest breathing zone and food surface.
- **Carving Surface:** Hardwood end-grain cutting block with deep perimeter juice groove to capture au jus runoff.

## 2. Electrical & Flame Hazards
- Use 12-gauge industrial extension cords with ground pin intact.
- Keep a 2.5 kg Class K wet chemical fire extinguisher mounted within 3 meters of any live heat carving station.
