# Banquet Waste Segregation, Composting & Ecological Disposal

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Sustainability SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Stewarding Staff, Banquet Captains, Logistics

---

## 1. Three-Stream Waste Sorting Station
Every back-of-house setup must deploy distinct color-coded receptacles:
- **Green (Biodegradable):** Leftover food scraps, vegetable trimmings, fruit peels (diverted to agricultural piggery feed partners).
- **Blue (Recyclable):** Clean plastic beverage bottles, aluminum soda cans, cardboard boxes.
- **Black (Residual Non-Recyclable):** Plastic cling film, soiled napkins, wax-coated paper.

## 2. Clean Venue Handover Policy
- Venue floors swept and mopped; zero food grease stains left on venue loading docks.
