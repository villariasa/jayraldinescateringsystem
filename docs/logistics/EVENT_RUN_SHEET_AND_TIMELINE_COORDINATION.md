## 1. Master Banquet Milestones (T-Clock)
- **T - 4:00 Hours:** Logistics truck arrives at venue; security gate pass presentation.
- **T - 3:00 Hours:** Physical table layout, linen placement, and centerpiece installation.
- **T - 2:00 Hours:** Buffet station setup, chafing dish water pan preheating.
- **T - 1:00 Hour:** Food transport Cambros arrive; hot food transferred to chafers.
- **T - 0:30 Hour:** Crew briefing, grooming check, ice filled in beverage dispensers.
- **T - 0:00 (Event Start):** Welcome drinks and cocktail hour station opened.
- **T + 1:30 Hours:** Main buffet opened following host prayer or welcome remarks.
- **T + 4:00 Hours:** Last call for buffet replenishment; transition to dessert station.
- **T + 5:00 Hours:** Teardown, trash consolidation, linen bagging, and truck reload.
