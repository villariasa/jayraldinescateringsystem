# Beverage Dispenser Refill Procedures & Ice Sanitation Standards

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Operations SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Bar Staff, Bussers, Banquet Servers

---

## 1. Dispenser Architecture & Spigot Care
Acrylic and glass beverage dispensers (Cucumber Lemonade, Four Seasons, Iced Tea) must be checked continuously:
- **Spigot Sanitization:** Clean spigot nozzle with food-safe alcohol wipe before each refill.
- **Drip Trays:** Empty stainless drip trays every 30 minutes to prevent sweet residue buildup and insect attraction.

## 2. Ice Handling Protocol
1. Ice must be transferred strictly using **dedicated NSF-certified polycarbonate ice scoops**.
2. Never submerge glass tumblers or pitcher rims into the ice chest to scoop ice.
3. The ice scoop must rest in a clean, sanitized hanging holster outside the ice container.
