# Chafing Dish Water Pan Preheating & Gel Fuel Burn Efficiency

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Banquet Setup Efficiency  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Banquet Servers, Kitchen Leads

---

## 1. The Preheating Requirement
Chafing fuel cans (methanol/ethanol gel) produce roughly 1,500 BTU/hr—sufficient to **maintain** hot food temperatures, but **insufficient to heat cold water**:
- Always fill water pans with pre-boiled hot water (minimum 80°C / 176°F) from kitchen urns.
- Maintain water depth between **20 mm and 25 mm** (approx. 1 inch). Overfilling causes boiling water to overflow when food pans are seated.

## 2. Fuel Can Lifespan & Wind Shielding
- Standard 200g gel can burn duration: **2.0 to 2.5 hours**.
- Inspect fuel wick height and canister dampers.
- On outdoor windy verandas, install magnetic wind collars around chafing frames to prevent flame deflection.
