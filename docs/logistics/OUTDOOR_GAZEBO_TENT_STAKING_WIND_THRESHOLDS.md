# Outdoor Gazebo Tent Staking Protocols & Wind Threshold Triggers

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Safety SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Setup Supervisors, Event Logistics

---

## 1. Ballast & Anchoring Standards
- **Grass / Soft Soil:** Heavy-duty 500mm steel rebar stakes driven at a 45-degree angle opposing tension lines.
- **Concrete / Paved Surfaces:** Heavy commercial water ballasts or 40 kg concrete block weights per tent leg.

## 2. Anemometer Wind Speed Thresholds

| Sustained Wind Speed | Status | Mandatory Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 15 knots (0 – 28 km/h) | Green (Normal) | Standard open sidewall setup |
| 16 – 25 knots (29 – 46 km/h) | Amber (Caution) | Drop and tie windward sidewalls, check all guy lines |
| 26 – 34 knots (47 – 63 km/h) | Orange (Warning) | Evacuate buffet food to indoor backup structure |
| **> 35 knots (> 64 km/h)** | **Red (Critical)** | **Immediate tent takedown and full guest relocation** |
