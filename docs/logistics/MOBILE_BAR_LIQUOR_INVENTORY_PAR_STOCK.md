# Mobile Bar Liquor Inventory Par Stocks & Wastage Log

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Beverage Operations Spec  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Head Bartender, Beverage Lead, Accounting

---

## 1. Par Stock Formulas per 100 Adult Guests (4-Hour Bar)
- **Standard Red / White Wine:** 25 bottles (approx. 125 glasses).
- **Spirits (Gin, Rum, Vodka):** 6 × 750ml bottles per category.
- **Mixers (Tonic, Soda Water, Cola):** 4 cases (96 cans).
- **Fresh Citrus & Garnishes:** 4 kg Calamansi / Limes, 1 jar maraschino cherries.

## 2. Empty Bottle Reconciled Return
- All empty spirit bottles must be retained in bar crates for inventory count reconciliation against POS drink tickets prior to disposal.
