# Outdoor Rain Contingency Canopy Deployment & Drainage Runoff

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Weather Emergency Plan  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Event Logistics, Banquet Leads, Setup Crew

---

## 1. Rain Gutter Interconnection
When placing multiple 6m × 6m pagoda or marquee tents side-by-side:
- Install heavy vinyl gutter strips between roof edges to prevent torrential waterfall runoff between tables.
- Route gutter spouts away from guest pathways and kitchen power generators.

## 2. Mud Mitigation & Flooring
- Lay corrugated rubber runners over natural turf pathways between catering staging tents and guest dining pavilions.
- Elevate food warming equipment minimum 150mm above ground level.
