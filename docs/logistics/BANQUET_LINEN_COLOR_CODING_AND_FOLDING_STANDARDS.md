# Banquet Linen Color Coding, Stacking & Napkin Folding Standards

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5

> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Table Setup Crew, Banquet Captains

---

## 1. Table Skirting & Linen Hierarchy
1. **Base Molleton / Silence Cloth:** Heavy felt under-cloth to dampen glassware clatter.
2. **Floor-Length Tablecloth:** Cream / White / Navy polyester tablecloth reaching precisely 1 inch above floor level.
3. **Table Runner / Overlay:** Coordinated accent silk or organza runner centered over guest tables.

## 2. Approved Napkin Folds
- **The Standing Fan:** Default formal fold for round guest tables.
- **The Bishop's Mitre:** Used exclusively for VIP Head Tables and Presidential Tables.
- **The Pocket Fold:** Used when inserting printed menu cards and custom favors.
