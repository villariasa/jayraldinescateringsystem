# Catering Crew Uniform Standards & Personal Grooming Checklist

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Crew Appearance SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** All Service Staff, Supervisors

---

## 1. Standard Uniform Components
- **Top:** Company-branded black collared polo, ironed, free of stains or tears.
- **Bottom:** Formal black tailored slacks (no jeans, leggings, or athletic sweatpants).
- **Footwear:** Closed-toe black leather or oil-resistant nonslip rubber service shoes with black socks.
- **Apron:** Half-waist black bistro apron equipped with order notepad, two black ink pens, and wine key.

## 2. Grooming & Hygiene Standards
- **Hair:** Hair past collar must be netted and pinned in a neat bun. Facial hair must be cleanly shaven or neatly trimmed to <5mm.
- **Jewelry:** Plain wedding band permitted. No hoop earrings, wristwatches, or bracelets during food handling.
- **Fragrance:** Heavy perfumes, colognes, or scented lotions are prohibited to avoid altering food aroma.
- **Hands:** Short, natural fingernails without artificial tips or polish.
