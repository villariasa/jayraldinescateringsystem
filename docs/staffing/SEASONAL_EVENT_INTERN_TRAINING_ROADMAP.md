## 1. Three-Week Rotation Roadmap
- **Week 1 (Commissary Prep & Knife Skills):** Vegetable cuts, sanitary storage, labeling systems, sauce base cooking.
- **Week 2 (Logistics & Equipment Rigging):** Chafing dish assembly, tent pitching, linen sizing, electrical load management.
- **Week 3 (Banquet Floor Service):** Table settings, plated service, beverage tray balancing, customer interaction.

Each intern is shadowed by an experienced senior server throughout their practicum.
