# Back-of-House Busser Tray Breakdown & Scraping Procedures

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Operations SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Bussers, Stewards, Captains

---

## 1. Back-of-House (BOH) Station Staging
Create a dedicated BOH clearing station screened by pipe-and-drape curtains away from guest view:

1. **Trash Bin 1:** Soft organics (food scraps scraped with silicone spatulas).
2. **Trash Bin 2:** Paper napkins, toothpicks, disposable cutlery.
3. **Soaking Tub:** Silverware submerged in lukewarm water with mild degreaser to prevent starch caking.
4. **Plate Racks:** Sorted by size (dinner plates, dessert plates, saucers) stacked no higher than 25 units per pile.

## 2. Tray Carry & Ergonomic Safety
- Maximum tray load: **12 dinner plates** per oval non-skid tray.
- Use tray jacks; never balance loaded service trays on dining tables or banquet guest chairs.
