# Formal Wine Presentation, Opening & Pouring Etiquette

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Fine Dining Service SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Banquet Servers, Bartenders

---

## 1. Presentation & Opening
1. Present wine bottle to host with label facing forward; recite vintage, winery, and varietal.
2. Cut foil under the bottom ring using wine key blade; wipe lip of bottle with clean cloth.
3. Extract cork smoothly without loud pop; inspect cork and place on small saucer beside host.

## 2. Pouring Technique
- Pour 30ml taste sample for host approval.
- Upon approval, serve guests clockwise (ladies first, host last), filling glasses to widest point of bowl (approx. 1/3 full).
- Twist bottle clockwise while lifting to prevent dripping on tablecloths.
