# Temporary Agency Crew Onboarding & Emergency Relief Roster

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** HR Operations Manual  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Operations Manager, Staffing Coordinator

---

## 1. Third-Party Staffing Agency Vetting
When peak wedding Saturdays exceed full-time staff bandwidth, partner agencies are activated subject to:
- Valid Barangay Clearance and Government ID on file.
- Completed Sanitary Permit / Food Handler Health Certificate.
- Verification of standard black banquet attire prior to vehicle dispatch.

## 2. On-Site Integration & Badge Allocation
1. Issue **Temporary Crew Badge (Yellow Tag)** denoting external roster.
2. Pair each agency worker with a senior full-time Jayraldine's server (Buddy System).
3. Agency crew are assigned to bussing, water replenishing, and teardown; captaincy and VIP plating remain strictly internal staff.
