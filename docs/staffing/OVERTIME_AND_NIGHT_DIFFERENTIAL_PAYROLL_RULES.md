# Banquet Crew Overtime & Night Shift Differential Payroll Rules

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Payroll & Compliance Spec  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Accounting, Payroll Lead, HR

---

## 1. Compliance with Philippine Labor Code (PD 442)
Banquet staff shifts frequently extend past standard hours due to late program conclusions and breakdown.

### 1.1 Regular Shift Allocation
- Standard event engagement: **8 hours** (including 2h setup, 4h service, 2h teardown/commissary return).

### 1.2 Overtime Calculation Formula
- Any hour worked exceeding 8 continuous hours is compensated at:
  $$	ext{OT Rate} = 	ext{Hourly Base} 	imes 1.25$$

### 1.3 Night Shift Differential (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM)
- Work rendered between 22:00 and 06:00 incurs an additional **10%** differential:
  $$	ext{Night Rate} = (	ext{Hourly Base} + 	ext{Applicable OT}) 	imes 1.10$$

## 2. Travel Allowance for Remote Venues
Venues located >25 km outside Cebu City proper (e.g., Danao, Carcar, Balamban) incur a fixed out-of-town per-diem of **₱350.00** per staff member in addition to van transport.
