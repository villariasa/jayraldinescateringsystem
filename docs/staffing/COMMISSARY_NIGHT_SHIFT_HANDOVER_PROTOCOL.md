# Commissary Night Shift Handover & Walk-In Chiller Lockup SOP

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Security & Operations SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Night Shift Chef, Closing Supervisor

---

## 1. Handover Checklist (02:00 AM Handover)
1. **Chiller Temperatures:** Verify Walk-In 1 (Proteins) at 2°C, Walk-In 2 (Produce) at 4°C.
2. **Gas Safety Valve:** Main LPG manifold master ball valve closed and locked with padlock.
3. **Extraction Hoods:** All kitchen hood blowers powered off; fire suppression system armed.
4. **Perimeter Locks:** External gates padlocked; biometric alarm system armed with central security dispatch.
