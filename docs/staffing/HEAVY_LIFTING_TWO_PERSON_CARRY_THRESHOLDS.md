# Runner Heavy Lifting Safety & Two-Person Carry Thresholds

## 1. Safe Lifting Rules
- Single person carry limit: $20\text{ kg}$ ($44\text{ lbs}$).
- Large soup stock pots ($50\text{L}$) and heavy round banquet tables ($60\text{ inches}$) mandate **Two-Person Team Lifts** using straight-back leg lift technique.
