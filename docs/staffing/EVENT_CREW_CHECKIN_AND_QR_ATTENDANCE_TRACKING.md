# Event Crew Digital QR Check-In & Automated Attendance Logging

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** HR Tech Specification  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** HR, Payroll, Banquet Captains

---

## 1. Digital Geofenced Attendance
- Service staff scan personalized QR code badges using Banquet Captain's tablet terminal.
- Terminal validates GPS coordinates within 200 meters of registered venue polygon before logging timestamp.

## 2. Shift Reconciliation
- Automatically imports verified service hours into Jayraldine's payroll engine, applying weekend and night differential multipliers.
