# Staff Voluntary Gratuity Pool Allocation & Transparent Disbursement

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Compensation Policy  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Accounting, Service Crew, Captains

---

## 1. 100% Pass-Through Rule
Voluntary tips provided by event hosts belong entirely to the event crew; company management deducts 0% administrative fees.

## 2. Transparent Point System
- Banquet Servers & Bussers: 1.0 Point.
- Kitchen Runners & Dish Stewards: 1.0 Point.
- Drivers & Logistics Loaders: 0.8 Point.
- Captain / Event Lead: 1.2 Points.

Points are tallied and cash disbursed immediately upon post-event commissary return, witnessed and signed by two crew representatives.
