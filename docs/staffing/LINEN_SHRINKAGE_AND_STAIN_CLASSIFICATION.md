# Banquet Linen Shrinkage & Stain Classification Standards

## 1. Stain Treatments
- Red wine & beetroot: Oxygenated enzymatic soak at $40^\circ\text{C}$.
- Wax & candle drippings: Warm iron blotting with parchment paper followed by degreaser wash.
- Heavily frayed linens retired into polishing rags.
