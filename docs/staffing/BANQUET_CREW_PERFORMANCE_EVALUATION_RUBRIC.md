# Banquet Crew Performance Evaluation Rubric & Merit Scoring

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** HR Performance System  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Supervisors, HR Manager

---

## 1. Evaluation Dimensions (1 to 5 Points Each)
1. **Punctuality & Grooming:** Arrives 15 mins prior to call time in immaculate uniform.
2. **Guest Engagement & Courtesy:** Warm professional greeting, respectful tone, proactive water service.
3. **Food Safety & Hygiene:** Adheres strictly to handwashing and glove usage protocols.
4. **Teamwork & Hustle:** Assists adjacent table runners during peak dish changeovers.
5. **Teardown Speed & Care:** Handled equipment carefully without china chipping or glass breakage.

High-scoring servers receive priority scheduling on premium wedding bookings.
