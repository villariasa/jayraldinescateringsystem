# Banquet Captain Pre-Service Event Briefing Procedures

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Operational Procedure  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Banquet Captains, Lead Supervisors, Floor Staff

---

## 1. Briefing Timeline (T minus 90 Minutes)
Held immediately after transport unload and initial buffet station table dressing:

1. **Attendance & Uniform Inspection (10 Mins):**
   - Verify black slacks, pressed company polo/apron, polished nonslip footwear, name tag.
   - Grooming check: hair tied in hairnets, clean trimmed nails, zero wrist jewelry.
2. **Event Profile Review (15 Mins):**
   - Review booking contract specifics: guest count, VIP table allocations, timeline (grand entrance, prayer, toast).
3. **Menu Breakdown & Allergen Alerts (15 Mins):**
   - Review each chafing dish assignment, set dish names, portioning ladles, and special dietary guest orders.
4. **Table Station Assignments (10 Mins):**
   - Designate zone captains, water replenishment runners, busser tray breakdown leads.
