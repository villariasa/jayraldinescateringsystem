# Field First Aid Kit Inventory & On-Site Incident Reporting SOP

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Emergency Response SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** All Staff, Banquet Captains

---

## 1. Mandatory Kit Contents
Every dispatch truck carries an inspected emergency first aid kit:
- Burn gel packets (Water-Jel) for hot chafing burner burns.
- Sterile gauze rolls and adhesive bandages.
- Antiseptic povidone-iodine swabs.
- Instant chemical cold packs.
- Digital bimetallic thermometer.

## 2. Incident Form Logging
Any injury must be logged within 2 hours in the digital tablet reporting module, noting injured employee name, incident description, and immediate treatment rendered.
