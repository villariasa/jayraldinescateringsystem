# Commissary & Event Crew Staff Meal Scheduling & Rest Policy

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Employee Welfare SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Kitchen Leads, Banquet Captains, All Staff

---

## 1. Shift Meal Entitlement
All staff assigned to shifts exceeding 5 continuous hours are entitled to a hot, nutritionally balanced commissary staff meal:

- **Commissary Prep Crew:** 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM (Staggered two batches).
- **Event Field Crew:** Scheduled strictly at **T minus 60 Minutes** prior to guest arrival.
  - Eating or drinking in guest banquet areas is strictly forbidden.
  - Meals are consumed in venue green rooms or back-of-house staging tents.

## 2. Hydration Guidelines
- Clean potable drinking water must be available in back-of-house dispensers with paper cups throughout hot outdoor setups.
- 10-minute hydration pauses scheduled every 2 hours in ambient temperatures >32°C.
