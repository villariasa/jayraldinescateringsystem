# Stewarding Field Dishwashing & Silverware Polishing Standard

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Quality Standards  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Stewarding Staff, Banquet Leads

---

## 1. On-Site Silverware Polishing
Every fork, spoon, and butter knife must undergo steam polishing before guest table setting:

1. Submerge washed cutlery in a clean stainless pitcher containing hot water (65°C+) with distilled white vinegar (10:1 ratio).
2. Remove using clean cotton handling gloves.
3. Buff with clean microfiber polishing cloths under inspection lighting.
4. Reject any piece showing water spots, tarnish, or clouding.

## 2. Chafing Dish Mirror Finish Restoration
- Stainless chafing roll-top lids must be polished with food-safe stainless steel cleaner on exterior surfaces only.
- Interior food contact wells must never receive chemical polish.
