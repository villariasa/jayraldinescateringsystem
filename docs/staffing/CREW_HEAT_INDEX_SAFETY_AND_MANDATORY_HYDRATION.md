# Crew Heat Index Safety Protocols & Mandatory Field Hydration

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Occupational Health SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** HR, Safety Officers, Banquet Captains

---

## 1. Heat Index Thresholds (PAGASA Reference)
- **33°C to 41°C (Extreme Caution):** Provide chilled electrolyte beverage in back-of-house; 15-minute shaded rest per 2 hours setup.
- **42°C to 51°C (Danger):** Rotate heavy outdoor loading tasks every 30 minutes; deploy portable misting fans in staff break staging areas.

## 2. First Aid Signs of Heat Exhaustion
- Dizziness, pale cool clammy skin, heavy sweating, nausea. Immediate removal to air-conditioned vehicle with cool wet compresses.
