# Catering Transport Van Dispatch Protocols & Night Driver Safety

> **Target Version:** v4.2.5  
> **Status:** Fleet Safety SOP  
> **Date:** October 8, 2026  
> **Audience:** Logistics Lead, Fleet Drivers, Operations

---

## 1. Fleet Vehicle Dispatch Sequencing
- **Van 1 (Equipment & Tableware):** Departs T minus 4.5 hours.
- **Van 2 (Hot Insulated Cambros & Perishables):** Departs T minus 2.5 hours.
- **Van 3 (Service Staff & Banquet Captain):** Departs T minus 2.0 hours.

## 2. Night Driver Safety Protocols (Post-Midnight Teardown)
1. **Mandatory Co-Pilot:** Drivers returning after 23:00 must have a designated awake co-pilot in the front passenger seat.
2. **Speed Restrictions:** Fleet maximum speed capped at 60 km/h on urban arteries and 40 km/h on mountain roads (e.g., Busay, Transcentral).
3. **Pre-Departure Circle Check:** Verify tire pressure, tie-down ratchet straps on heavy chafing road cases, and rear hazard lights.
