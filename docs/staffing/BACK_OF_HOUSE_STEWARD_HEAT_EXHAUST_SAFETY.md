# Back-of-House Dish Stewarding Heat Exhaust Safety & PPE

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Workplace Safety Standard  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Stewarding Staff, Facilities Manager

---

## 1. Thermal & Chemical PPE
Dishwashing stewards operating high-temperature sinks must wear:
- Heavy-duty elbow-length nitrile or neoprene chemical gloves.
- Waterproof heavy vinyl bib aprons.
- Steel-toe oil-resistant slip-resistant safety boots.

## 2. Ventilation Requirements
Continuous 1200 CFM exhaust canopy active over dish machine to exhaust steam and prevent atmospheric heat buildup above 30°C.
