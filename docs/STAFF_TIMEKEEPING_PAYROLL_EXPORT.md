# Banquet Staff Timekeeping & Payroll Export Formatting

## 1. Shift Wage Computation
- **Standard Event Shift**: 6-hour baseline pay.
- **Overtime Rate**: $1.25\times$ hourly rate for shifts exceeding 6 hours.
- **Night Differential**: $1.10\times$ multiplier for banquet service extending past 10:00 PM.
- **Automated Export**: Generates bank-ready payroll summary workbooks with employee SSS/PhilHealth/TIN records.
