# Catering Equipment & Rental Deposit Tracking Specification

## 1. Managed Equipment Assets
- Chafing dish sets, fuel burners, and serving spoons.
- Fine porcelain dinner plates, goblets, and flatware.
- Banquet round tables, tiffany chairs, and table linens.
- Sound systems, microphones, and stage lighting units.

## 2. Deposit & Breakage Accounting
- Security deposits are collected upfront and recorded in `rental_deposits`.
- Returned items are inspected during egress; broken or missing assets deduct from deposit at replacement value.
