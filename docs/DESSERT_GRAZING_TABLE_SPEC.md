# Dessert Grazing Table Staging & Styling Specification

## 1. Styling Elements
- Tiered acrylic, wooden, and gold risers creating multi-level visual depth.
- Decorative foliage (eucalyptus, baby's breath) accenting food display platters.
- Glass cloches protecting delicate cream pastries from dust and drafts.

## 2. Temperature Management
Chocolate ganache and whipped cream items are held in portable chillers until 15 minutes prior to guest unveiling.
