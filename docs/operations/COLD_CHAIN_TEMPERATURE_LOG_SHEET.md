# Commissary & Transport Cold Chain Temperature Log Sheet

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active Standard  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Food Safety, HACCP Compliance, Commissary Kitchen

---

## 1. Temperature Standards

| Food Category | Max Commissary Storage Temp | Max In-Transit Temp | Serving Temp |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw Beef, Pork, Poultry | 4.0°C (Chiller) / -18.0°C (Freezer) | 4.0°C (Insulated Icebox) | Cooked to 74°C internal |
| Fresh Seafood (Shrimp, Fish) | 2.0°C (Slush Ice) | 2.0°C (Packed on Ice) | Cooked to 68°C internal |
| Cooked Dishes (Hot Holding) | N/A (Hot Hold > 60°C) | > 60°C (Cambro Box) | > 60°C in Chafing Dish |
| Cold Desserts (Salad, Cake) | 4.0°C | 4.0°C | < 7°C on Ice Buffet |

## 2. In-Transit Verification Log
Every delivery truck carries calibrated digital probe thermometers. The lead logistics officer must record departure temperature and arrival temperature at venue ingress.\n
