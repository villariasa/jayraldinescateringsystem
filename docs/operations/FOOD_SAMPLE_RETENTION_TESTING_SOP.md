# Commissary Food Sample Retention & Quality Testing SOP

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active SOP  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Food Safety, Quality Assurance, Laboratory Sampling

---

## 1. Purpose
To protect the catering business against unsubstantiated food poisoning claims and ensure microbial safety, retention samples are collected from every served menu item.

---

## 2. Sampling Protocol

1. **Portion Size:** 100g sample of each cooked dish taken directly from the prep batch before departure.
2. **Container:** Sterile food-grade specimen containers labeled with Date, Dish Name, Batch ID, and Event Reference.
3. **Retention Period:** Samples refrigerated at 2.0°C for **72 hours** post-event.
4. **Disposal / Testing:** If zero client feedback occurs within 72 hours, samples are safely autoclaved/discarded.\n
