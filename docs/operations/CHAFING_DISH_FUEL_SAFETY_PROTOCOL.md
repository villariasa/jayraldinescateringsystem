# Chafing Dish Fuel & Open Flame Safety Protocol

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active Runbook  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Banquet Service, Commissary Safety, Field Logistics

---

## 1. Purpose
Chafing dish canned fuels (Sterno methanol/ethanol/diethylene glycol) present fire and burn risks during catering events. This standard operating procedure guarantees safe buffet heating.

---

## 2. Handling & Storage Protocols

1. **Storage Standards:**
   - Canned fuels must be stored in cool, ventilated chemical lockers away from gas stoves and direct sunlight.
   - Temperature must not exceed 35°C in commissary transit vehicles.
2. **Buffet Setup Rules:**
   - Water pans must have at least 1 inch of boiling water **before** lighting fuel cans.
   - Never light empty chafing dishes.
   - Cans must be positioned strictly within designated chafer fuel holders.
3. **Lighting & Extinguishing:**
   - Use long-reach butane lighters. Never use matches or paper tapers.
   - Extinguish by sliding the fuel holder snuffer lid over the flame. **Never blow on a canned fuel flame.**\n
