# Commissary Kitchen Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Standard

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active Standard  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Kitchen Sanitation, DOH & FDA Compliance, Facility Audit

---

## 1. Facility Pest Exclusion Barriers
1. **Air Curtains:** Operational air curtains over all loading docks and commissary entrances.
2. **Drain Grates:** Stainless steel micro-mesh grates on all floor wastewater lines.
3. **Sealed Openings:** Zero openings exceeding 6mm along external walls and door sweeps.

---

## 2. Professional Extermination Schedule
- Monthly professional pest control treatments (quaternary pest bait stations outside food production perimeters).
- Daily inspection logs for rodent or insect activity.\n
