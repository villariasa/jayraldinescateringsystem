# Event Timeline Management for Multi-Course Plated Banquets

## Synchronization Milestones:
- **T - 45 min**: Bread service & cold salads pre-set on guest tables.
- **T + 00 min**: Grand Entrance & Welcome Remarks.
- **T + 20 min**: Plated hot soup course served.
- **T + 50 min**: Main entree synchronized synchronized tray service.
- **T + 90 min**: Dessert course and coffee/tea station opening.
