# Catering Transport Fleet Preventive Maintenance Guide

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active Guide  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Fleet Management, Logistics, Vehicle Inspection

---

## 1. Vehicle Inspection Checklist (Daily Pre-Trip)

- **Tire Pressure & Tread:** Inspect all tires including spare tire.
- **Coolant & Engine Oil:** Verify fluid levels before loading heavy equipment.
- **Cargo Bay Sanitation:** Cargo floor must be pressure-washed and sanitized with food-safe quaternary ammonium solution.
- **Cargo Tie-Down Anchors:** Ratchet straps and cargo nets checked for frays.

---

## 2. Maximum Payload Constraints
- **L300 / Aluminum Van:** Max 1,200 kg (approx. 200-pax equipment + food).
- **Light Truck (4-Wheeler):** Max 2,500 kg (up to 400-pax setup).\n
