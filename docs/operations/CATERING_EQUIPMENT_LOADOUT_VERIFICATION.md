## 1. Departure Gate Verification
Before transport vehicle leaves commissary gates, the logistics supervisor must scan and verify all itemized equipment against the **Manifest Slip**:

- [ ] Dinner Plates (Expected: Pax + 15% buffer)
- [ ] Chafing Dish Sets (Frames, water pans, food inserts, lids)
- [ ] Serving Tongs, Ladles & Spoons
- [ ] Water Pitchers & Beverage Dispensers
- [ ] Table Linen Bundles (Round table cloths, top toppers, skirting)
- [ ] Trash Bins & Heavy-Duty Garbage Bags (Minimum 10 rolls)
- [ ] First Aid Kit & ABC Dry Chemical Fire Extinguisher\n
