## 1. Traffic Engineering Principles

Queue bottlenecks at buffets frustrate guests and cause cold food. Flow rate optimization adheres to the following thresholds:

| Guest Count (Pax) | Recommended Line Configuration | Service Speed (Guests/min) |
|---|---|---|
| 50 – 80 Pax | Single-Sided Buffet Line | 8 – 10 |
| 100 – 150 Pax | Double-Sided Buffet Line | 18 – 22 |
| 200+ Pax | Dual Island Buffet Stations | 35 – 40 |

## 2. Table Sequencing Standard

```
[Start] Plates & Flatware -> Rice -> Vegetables -> Pasta -> Pork -> Beef -> Chicken -> Seafood -> Rolls -> [End]
```

- **Plates First:** Elevated stacks of 25 plates maximum to prevent tipping.
- **Carbs Before Proteins:** Rice and vegetables placed first to naturally portion high-cost meats.
- **Desserts & Beverages Separated:** Always station dessert buffets and beverage stations at separate tables away from the main meal queue.\n
