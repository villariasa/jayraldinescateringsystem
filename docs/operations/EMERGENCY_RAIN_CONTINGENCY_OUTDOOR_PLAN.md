## 1. Weather Monitoring (T-minus 6 Hours)
The event coordinator tracks PAGASA satellite weather radar for incoming rainfall fronts or tropical depressions in Metro Cebu.

---

## 2. Protocol Levels

### Level 1: Overcast / High Humidity (50% Rain Probability)
- Pre-position waterproof tarpaulins over all buffet table frames.
- Stage backup indoor layout plan with venue management.

### Level 2: Active Drizzle / Onset of Rain
- Execute **Emergency Plan B**: relocate food warmers and chaffing lines to covered veranda or indoor hall within 15 minutes.
- Cover all electrical audio/sound plugs and decorative lights immediately.\n
