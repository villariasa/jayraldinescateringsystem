# Beverage Station Replenishment Protocols & Ice Par Levels

## Formula:
$$\text{Ice Required (kg)} = \text{Pax} \times 0.75 \text{ kg (Indoors)} \quad \lor \quad \text{Pax} \times 1.25 \text{ kg (Outdoors)}$$
Replenishment runners check cooler bin levels every 30 minutes during peak reception hours.
