> **Target Version:** v4.2.4

---

## 1. Overview
At high-tier weddings and corporate banquets, the presidential or VIP table receives plated silver service rather than self-service buffet queuing.

---

## 2. Butler Protocols

1. **Service Side:**
   - Serve food from the **left** side of the guest using left hand.
   - Serve beverages and wine from the **right** side with right hand.
   - Clear plates from the **right** side once silverware is placed in finished position (4:20 clock angle).
2. **Beverage Maintenance:**
   - Wine and water goblets must never fall below 1/3 full.
   - Continuous replenishment without interrupting conversation.\n
