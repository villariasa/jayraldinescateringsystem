# Commercial Kitchen Fire Safety & Emergency Evacuation

## 1. Safety Systems
- **Extinguisher Types**: Class K wet chemical extinguishers stationed adjacent to deep fryers and roasting stations; Class ABC extinguishers at entry doors.
- **Automatic LPG Shutoff**: Solenoid gas shut-off valves linked to combustible gas leak detectors.
- **Quarterly Drills**: All culinary and service staff trained in emergency evacuation routes and burn first-aid.
