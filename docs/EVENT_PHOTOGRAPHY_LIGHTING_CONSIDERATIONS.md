# Buffet Table Illumination & Food Presentation Lighting

## 1. Lighting Specifications
- **Color Rendering Index (CRI)**: $\ge 95$ high-CRI warm white LED spotlights ($3000\text{K}$) to enhance the natural colors of roasted meats and fresh dishes.
- **Glare Reduction**: Diffused food-safe warming lamps positioned at $45^{\circ}$ angles over carving boards.
