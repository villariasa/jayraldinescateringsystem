# HACCP Food Safety & Temperature Logging Controls

## 1. Critical Temperature Standards
- **Chilled Storage**: Cold holding must maintain $\le 4^{\circ}\text{C}$ ($40^{\circ}\text{F}$).
- **Cooking Temperatures**: Poultry internal $\ge 74^{\circ}\text{C}$ ($165^{\circ}\text{F}$); Pork/Beef $\ge 68^{\circ}\text{C}$ ($155^{\circ}\text{F}$).
- **Buffet Hot Holding**: Chafing dishes must maintain food temperature $\ge 60^{\circ}\text{C}$ ($140^{\circ}\text{F}$) throughout service.
- **Service Time Limit**: 4-hour maximum exposure window for prepared perishable buffet items.
