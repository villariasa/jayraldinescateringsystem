# Banquet Table Linens Inventory & Care Standards

## 1. Standard Linen Sizes
- **Round Table Cloth (120-inch)**: Designed for 60-inch (10-seater) round tables with floor-length drop.
- **Buffet Skirting (14-foot & 21-foot)**: Pleated satin skirts with velcro clips for buffet staging.
- **Table Runners & Cloth Napkins**: Premium cotton-blend in signature Navy, Gold, Burgundy, and Sage Green.

## 2. Stain Removal Protocol
Pre-treat oily gravy and wine spots with enzyme detergents within 6 hours of event egress.
