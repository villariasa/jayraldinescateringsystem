# Banquet Kitchen Preparation Sheet Specification

## 1. Purpose
Translates front-of-house booking contracts into kitchen production schedules for chefs and cooks.

## 2. Metrics Calculated
- Total Portions: $\text{Pax} \times \text{Serving Multiplier}$.
- Ingredient Aggregation: Sums identical raw materials across multiple concurrent events on the same date.
- Cook Timelines: Categorizes cold appetizers, hot entrees, and live roasting stations.
