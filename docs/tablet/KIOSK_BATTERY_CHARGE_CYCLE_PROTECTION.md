# Kiosk Tablet Battery Health Management & Charge Cycle Protection

> **Target Version:** v2.7.5  
> **Status:** Hardware Operations SOP  
> **Date:** October 29, 2026  
> **Audience:** Facilities Techs, Hardware Maintenance

---

## 1. Continuous AC Power Risks
Kiosk tablets mounted in acrylic enclosures continuously connected to 5V USB power supplies risk lithium battery swelling and thermal degradation.

## 2. Mitigation Policies
1. **Smart Plug Cycle Scheduling:** Connect kiosk enclosures to smart timer plugs programmed to cycle power off between 02:00 AM and 06:00 AM daily.
2. **Android Battery Protect Mode:** Enable OEM charge threshold capping (stop charging at 80% capacity).
3. **Monthly Visual Inspection:** Inspect acrylic enclosures bi-weekly for backplate bulging or screen lift.
