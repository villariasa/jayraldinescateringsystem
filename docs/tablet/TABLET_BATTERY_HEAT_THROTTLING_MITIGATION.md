# Tablet Battery Temperature Throttling Mitigation in Outdoor Heat

## 1. Hardware Safeguards
When ambient marquee temperature exceeds $35^\circ\text{C}$ and battery temperature exceeds $42^\circ\text{C}$:
- Screen refresh rate capped at 60Hz.
- Standby screen brightness dimmed by 25%.
- Aluminum heat-dissipating kiosk mount brackets utilized.
