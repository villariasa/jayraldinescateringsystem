# Tablet Kiosk Screen Idle Timeout & Battery Management Specification

> **Target Version:** v1.2.8  
> **Status:** Approved / Active Specification  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Tablet Android APK, Battery Management, Kiosk Mode

---

## 1. Purpose
During 8-hour outdoor catering setups without direct AC wall outlets, tablet battery preservation is essential.

---

## 2. Idle Timeout Policy
- After **3 minutes** of inactivity, screen brightness dims to 20%.
- After **10 minutes** of inactivity, screen sleeps into a low-power listening state.
- Instant wake upon screen touch or receipt print trigger.\n
