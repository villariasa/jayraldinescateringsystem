## 1. Acoustic Feedback Design
- **Item Added to Order:** Subtle high-pitch click (440 Hz, 50ms).
- **Payment Success:** Harmonious chime chord (C5-E5-G5, 200ms).
- **Sync Error / Alert:** Gentle double low-tone beep (220 Hz).\n
