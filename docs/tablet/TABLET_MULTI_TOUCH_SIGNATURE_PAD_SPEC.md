## 1. Requirements
For on-site contract acceptance, clients sign directly on the tablet screen.
- Smooth Bézier curve interpolation for natural handwritten signatures.
- Anti-aliased canvas rendering.
- Vector PNG export compressed to under 25 KB.\n
