# Supplier Purchase Order Cycle & Accounts Payable

## 1. Procurement Lifecycle
1. **Purchase Requisition**: Auto-generated from ingredient yield calculations against upcoming events.
2. **Vendor RFQ**: Generates price comparison sheets across registered meat and produce vendors.
3. **Goods Inward Inspection**: Inspects weight, freshness, and temperature of delivered goods before approving warehouse storage.
4. **Payment Voucher**: Dispatches accounts payable voucher matching agreed vendor credit terms (Net 15 / Net 30).
