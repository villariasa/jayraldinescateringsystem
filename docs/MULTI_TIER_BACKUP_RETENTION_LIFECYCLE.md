# Grandfather-Father-Son (GFS) Database Backup Retention Policy

## 1. Backup Schedule & Lifespan
- **Daily Backups (Son)**: Retained locally for 14 days.
- **Weekly Backups (Father)**: Generated every Sunday at midnight; retained for 8 weeks.
- **Monthly Backups (Grandfather)**: Generated on the 1st of every month; retained for 5 years in encrypted offsite storage.
