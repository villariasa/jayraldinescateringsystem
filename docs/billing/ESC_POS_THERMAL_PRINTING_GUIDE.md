# ESC/POS 80mm Thermal Receipt Printing Guide

## 1. Output Format
- Header: Centered catering business name, logo bitmap, contact info.
- Divider: Dotted rule (`--------------------------------`).
- Itemized Table: Dish/Package description, pax count, unit price, line total.
- Summary: Subtotal, Downpayment Paid, Balance Remaining.
- Footer: Return/Cancellation policy notice and customer signature line.
