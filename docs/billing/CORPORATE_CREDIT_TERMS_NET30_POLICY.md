# Corporate Client Credit Line & Net-30 Invoicing Policy

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active Policy  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Credit Accounts, Corporate Billing, Accounts Receivable

---

## 1. Credit Terms Eligibility
Corporate clients, universities, and government agencies with recurring banquet contracts may apply for **Net-15** or **Net-30** billing privileges after submitting:
- SEC / DTI Registration Certificate
- BIR Form 2303 Certificate of Registration
- Verified Corporate Purchase Order (PO) signed by Finance Director

---

## 2. Overdue Escalation Workflow
- **Day 31:** Automated statement of account sent via email.
- **Day 45:** Telephone follow-up by Accounts Receivable lead.
- **Day 60:** Suspension of catering booking privileges until account settlement.\n
