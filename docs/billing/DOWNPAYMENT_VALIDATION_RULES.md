# Downpayment Validation & Cancellation Terms

## 1. Reservation Deposit Policy
- Standard catering reservations require a minimum deposit (e.g., 20% to 50%) to transition from `Tentative` to `Confirmed`.
- Bookings without a deposit within 48 hours of reservation trigger automated calendar expiration warnings.

## 2. Cancellation Terms
- Cancellations made > 14 days before the event are eligible for partial deposit refund.
- Cancellations within 7 days forfeit the deposit to cover food procurement costs.
