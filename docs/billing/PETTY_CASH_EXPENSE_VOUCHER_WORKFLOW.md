# Petty Cash Fund Disbursement & Voucher Reconciliation

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active SOP  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Cash Flow, Petty Cash Drawer, Expense Logging

---

## 1. Petty Cash Fund Structure
- **Custodian:** Commissary Head Cashier.
- **Base Fund Amount:** PHP 10,000.00 cash float.
- **Maximum Single Voucher:** PHP 3,000.00. (Disbursements above PHP 3,000 require check/bank payment).

---

## 2. Voucher Workflow
1. Requester fills digital Petty Cash Voucher on Desktop app.
2. Custodian disburses physical cash and logs official receipt attachment.
3. When fund balance falls below PHP 2,500.00, a Replenishment Check is issued against Bank Checking Account.\n
