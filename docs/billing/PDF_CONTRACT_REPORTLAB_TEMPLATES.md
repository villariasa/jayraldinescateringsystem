# ReportLab PDF Contract Vector Layout Specification

## 1. Document Structure
- **Page Size**: Letter / A4 with 0.5-inch margins.
- **Typography**: Helvetica / Helvetica-Bold with clean hierarchical point sizes.
- **Sections**:
  1. Business Header & Document ID.
  2. Client Information & Event Venue Schedule.
  3. Package Details & Complimentary Inclusions.
  4. Payment Terms & Breakdown.
  5. Terms & Conditions Agreement with Dual Signatures.
