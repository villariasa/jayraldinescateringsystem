# Booking Cancellation Penalty & Refund Fee Schedule

> **Target Version:** v4.2.4  
> **Status:** Approved / Active Policy  
> **Date:** October 7, 2026  
> **Component:** Contracts, Refund Processing, Customer Accounts

---

## 1. Penalty Schedule

| Cancellation Notice Window | Downpayment Refund | Security Deposit Refund |
|---|---|---|
| More than 30 Days before Event | 50% Refundable | 100% Full Refund |
| 15 to 30 Days before Event | Non-Refundable (Credit Valid for 6 Months) | 100% Full Refund |
| Less than 14 Days before Event | Forfeited in Full | 100% Full Refund |
| Less than 48 Hours before Event | Full Contract Amount Due | Forfeited for ingredients purchased |

---

## 2. Force Majeure Clause
In verified cases of Super Typhoons (Signal #3 or higher in Cebu) or government pandemic lockdowns, rebooking fees are waived completely.\n
