# Kitchen & Event First Aid Safety Runbook

## 1. First Aid Kit Inventory
Every catering logistics vehicle carries a certified first aid kit containing:
- Hydrogel sterile burn dressings.
- Waterproof adhesive bandages and sterile gauze rolls.
- Antiseptic povidone-iodine swabs.
- Burn relief spray and electrolyte rehydration salts.

## 2. Emergency Escalation
In the event of a severe burn or deep cut, the Banquet Captain immediately escorts the staff member to the nearest medical facility.
