# Banquet Equipment & Breakage Reconciliation Engine

## 1. Inventory & Breakage Tracking
Manages catering assets deployed to external event venues:

- **Asset Classes**: Chafing dishes, beverage dispensers, high-grade cutlery, porcelain dinnerware, glass goblets, chafing fuel burners.
- **Security Deposits**: Calculates required refundable security deposits based on guest count and equipment tier.
- **Breakage Auditing**: Logs broken or missing items during event egress with automatic deduction from customer deposits.
