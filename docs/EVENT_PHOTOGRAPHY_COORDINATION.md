# Banquet Photography Coordination & Food Staging Guide

## 1. Pre-Opening Photo Window
A dedicated 10-minute window is reserved before buffet ribbon-cutting for official event photographers to capture pristine, untouched buffet tablescapes.

## 2. Lighting & Presentation
All chafing dish lid windows are wiped clear of condensation, and garnish sprigs are freshly misted with water for optimal photo sheen.
