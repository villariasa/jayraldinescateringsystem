# Roaming Butler Cocktail & Hors d'Oeuvres Service Guide

## 1. Butler Service Rules
- Waiters hold service platters with their left hand and cocktail napkins in their right hand.
- Maintain consistent roaming cadence through the reception foyer.
- Clear empty toothpick skewers and discarded cocktail glasses continuously.

## 2. Portion Ratios
Calculate 4 to 6 hors d'oeuvres pieces per guest per hour of pre-dinner cocktail reception.
