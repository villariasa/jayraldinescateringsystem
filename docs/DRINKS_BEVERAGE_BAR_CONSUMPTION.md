# Beverage Station Consumption Metrics & Refill Protocols

## 1. Consumption Estimation
- **Average Guest Consumption**: $2.5\text{ glasses}$ ($625\text{ mL}$) of chilled beverage per guest for a 3-hour event.
- **Gallon Conversion**: 1 Gallon ($3.785\text{ L}$) serves approximately 15 guests.
- **Flavor Distribution**: $60\%$ House Blend Citrus Iced Tea, $40\%$ Cucumber Lemonade / Tropical Fruit Punch.
