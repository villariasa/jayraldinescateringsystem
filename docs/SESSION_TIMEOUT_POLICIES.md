# Session Timeout Policies & Terminal Locking Specification

## 1. Inactivity Thresholds
- **Front-of-House POS Terminal**: Locks after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- **Manager / Administrator Station**: Locks after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- **Tablet Kiosk**: Returns to main welcome screen after 3 minutes of guest inactivity.

## 2. Re-Authentication
Unlocking a locked station requires entering the user's PIN or password without terminating in-progress booking forms.
