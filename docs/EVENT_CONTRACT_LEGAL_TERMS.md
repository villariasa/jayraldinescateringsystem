# Standard Catering Agreement Legal Provisions & Terms

## 1. Contractual Terms
- **Reservation Commitment**: Bookings are tentative until initial deposit is received.
- **Final Headcount Lock**: Confirmed guest count must be finalized 7 business days before the event date.
- **Cancellation & Refunds**:
  - > 30 days notice: 80% deposit refund.
  - 15-30 days notice: 50% deposit refund.
  - < 15 days notice: Deposit non-refundable.
