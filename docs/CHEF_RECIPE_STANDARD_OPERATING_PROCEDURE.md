# Commissary Master Recipe SOP & Portioning Standards

## 1. Recipe Standardization Controls
- **Standardized Batch Cards**: Ingredient weights recorded in metric grams and milliliters.
- **Culinary Quality Milestones**: Step-by-step cooking temperatures, seasoning balance checkpoints, and visual consistency benchmarks.
- **Portion Controls**: Calibrated ladles and serving spoons to ensure exact per-pax distribution.
