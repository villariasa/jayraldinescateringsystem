# Post-Event Customer Feedback & Sentiment Analysis Pipeline

## 1. Survey Dispatch
Automated feedback request emails and SMS links are triggered 24 hours post-event completion.

## 2. Evaluation Metrics
- Food Quality & Taste (1-5 stars)
- Presentation & Setup Aesthetics (1-5 stars)
- Staff Courtesy & Service Speed (1-5 stars)
- Overall Banquet Satisfaction (1-5 stars)

## 3. AI Sentiment Processing
Chef Jay AI analyzes free-form customer comments, tagging positive praise and critical improvement suggestions.
