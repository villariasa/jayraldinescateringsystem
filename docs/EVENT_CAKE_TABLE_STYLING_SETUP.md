# Wedding & Debut Cake Table Setup Standards

## 1. Setup Guidelines
- **Structural Integrity**: Heavy-duty reinforced cake tables capable of supporting multi-tier fondant cakes ($25+\text{kg}$).
- **Spotlighting**: Warm white directional LED pin-spots focused on the master cake tiers.
- **Cake Knife & Champagne**: Silver-plated cake knife and twin toasting crystal flutes arranged with satin ribbon accents.
