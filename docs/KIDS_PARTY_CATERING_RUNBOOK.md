# Children's Party & Milestone Celebration Catering Runbook

## 1. Kid-Friendly Menu Selections
- Sweet-style Filipino spaghetti with chicken hotdog slices and grated cheddar.
- Crispy golden chicken tenders with honey-mustard and gravy dipping sauces.
- Mini beef burgers on toasted sesame buns.
- Assorted cupcakes and colorful gelatin cups.

## 2. Safety Guidelines
All children's station tableware uses shatterproof, food-grade melamine rather than porcelain.
