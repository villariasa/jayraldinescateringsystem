# Catering Waste Segregation & Environmental Management

## 1. Waste Management Hierarchy
- **Biodegradable Organic Waste**: Scraped food waste collected in heavy-duty green bins for agricultural compost processing.
- **Recyclable Packaging**: Aluminum foil, metal beverage cans, and cardboard boxes sorted for recycling.
- **Residual Waste**: Minimal residual landfill disposal following environmental compliance laws (RA 9003).
