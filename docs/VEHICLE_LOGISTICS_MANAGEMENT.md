## 2. LIFO Loading Order
- **First Out**: Cooking burners, heavy tables, and chafing dish frames.
- **Second Out**: Table linens, chinaware, flatware, and glassware.
- **Last Out (Direct to Station)**: Insulated cambros containing hot food pans.
