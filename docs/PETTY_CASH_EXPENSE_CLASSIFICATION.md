# Petty Cash & Operating Expense Classification

## 1. Expense Taxonomies
- **Market & Commissary**: Fresh produce, meats, spices, cooking gas (LPG).
- **Logistics & Fuel**: Diesel/gasoline allocations, expressway toll fees, parking permits.
- **Equipment & Maintenance**: Kitchen utensil replacements, burner repairs, vehicle maintenance.
- **Operational Petty Cash**: Event venue emergency supplies, ice replenishment, staff refreshments.
