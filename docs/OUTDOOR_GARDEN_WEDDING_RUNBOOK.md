# Outdoor Garden Wedding Contingency Runbook

## 1. Weather Contingency Plans
- **Wind Protection**: Heavyweight tablecloth clips and wind-guard shields around all chafing dish burners.
- **Rain Contingency**: Mandatory high-peak canopy tent setup over buffet tables with waterproof sidewalls.
- **Ground Protection**: Plywood runner paths laid over damp grass to protect guest footwear and catering trolleys.
