# Client Food Tasting Session Protocol & Customization Workflow

## 1. Tasting Session Setup
- Conducted at the central commissary tasting lounge for weddings and major corporate accounts (100+ pax).
- Chef Jay prepares 4 sample entrees, 2 appetizers, and 2 desserts in standard presentation portions.

## 2. Critique Sheet
The client rates seasoning, tenderness, and presentation, recording adjustments (e.g., "reduce sweetness in sauce") directly into the system.
