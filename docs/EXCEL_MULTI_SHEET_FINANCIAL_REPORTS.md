# OpenPyXL Multi-Sheet Financial Workbook Architecture

## 1. Workbook Structure
- **Sheet 1 (`Summary`)**: Executive dashboard metrics, monthly revenue chart, gross profit margin.
- **Sheet 2 (`Bookings`)**: Comprehensive ledger of all confirmed events with itemized pax counts and package choices.
- **Sheet 3 (`Receivables`)**: Outstanding balances aging report categorized by 30/60/90 days.
- **Sheet 4 (`Expenses`)**: Itemized operational expenses categorized by commissary, logistics, and payroll.
