# Banquet Staff Uniform & Grooming Standards

## 1. Dress Code Specifications
- **Banquet Servers**: Crisp black long-sleeved button-down shirt, black dress trousers, black bowtie, polished non-slip leather shoes.
- **Kitchen Crew**: White double-breasted chef coats, black houndstooth kitchen trousers, breathable chef skull caps, hair restraints.
- **Hygiene**: Clean-shaven, trimmed fingernails, no dangling jewelry during food handling.
