# Catering Contract Legal Provisions & Force Majeure Guidelines

## 1. Contract Terms
- **Cancellation Schedule**:
  - $>30\text{ days}$ prior: $100\%$ refund less administrative booking fee.
  - $15-30\text{ days}$ prior: $50\%$ refund of down payment.
  - $<15\text{ days}$ prior: Non-refundable down payment.
- **Force Majeure**: Typhoon storm signal declarations, government travel restrictions, or natural calamities allow date rebooking within 6 months at zero penalty.
