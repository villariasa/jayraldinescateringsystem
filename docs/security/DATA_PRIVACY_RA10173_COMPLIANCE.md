# Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) Compliance Guide

## 1. Personal Data Collected
- Client full names, contact numbers, and delivery/venue addresses.
- Payment transaction histories.

## 2. Protective Safeguards
- Data is stored locally behind LAN firewalls with no unauthorized third-party telemetry.
- Passwords and sensitive keys are salted and hashed.
- Client contact lists are accessible only to authorized managerial roles.
- Customers have the right to request deletion or correction of booking records upon full settlement.
