# Standardized Recipe Yield Cards & Commissary Batch Scaling

## 1. Recipe Multiplier Algorithm
 commissary recipe yields are standardized to 50-pax base batches:
$$\text{Batch Multiplier} = \frac{\text{Confirmed Event Pax}}{50}$$

## 2. Seasoning Adjustment Curve
Spices and salt follow a logarithmic tapering curve at large scale (> 500 pax) to prevent over-seasoning during high-volume cooking.
