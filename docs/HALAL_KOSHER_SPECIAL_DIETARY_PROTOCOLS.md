# Special Dietary Protocols & Utensil Segregation

## 1. Segregation Standards
- **Dedicated Cookware**: Dedicated stainless steel pots, cutting boards, and knives reserved exclusively for Halal / Vegetarian dish preparation.
- **Pork-Free Separation**: Zero cross-contact with pork products or cooking oils.
- **Certified Ingredients**: Sourcing certified Halal poultry and beef with supplier inspection certificates.
