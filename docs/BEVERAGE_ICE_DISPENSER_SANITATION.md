# Commercial Ice Machine Sanitation & Filtration Standards

## 1. Sanitation Protocol
- **Water Filtration**: 3-stage carbon block and sediment filtration ($0.5\text{ micron}$).
- **Monthly De-scaling**: Acid-based de-scaling flush of evaporator plates.
- **Scoop Handling**: Stainless steel ice scoops stored in dedicated sanitized hanging holders outside the ice bin.
