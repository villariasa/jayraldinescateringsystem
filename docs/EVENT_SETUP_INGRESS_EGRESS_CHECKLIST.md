# Banquet Ingress & Egress Operational Checklists

## 1. Ingress Phase (T - 2.5 Hours)
- [ ] Verify venue power outlets and water supply access.
- [ ] Assemble buffet tables, linen skirting, and food warmers.
- [ ] Set up chafing fuel burners and pre-heat hot holding trays.
- [ ] Inspect dinnerware cleanliness and arrange cutlery placements.

## 2. Egress Phase (T + 4.0 Hours)
- [ ] Package leftover food into client takeaway containers.
- [ ] Extinguish chafing fuel burners and allow hot water pans to cool.
- [ ] Count dinnerware and glassware against packing manifest.
- [ ] Conduct final venue sweep and sign off client satisfaction sheet.
