# Banquet Staff Allocation & Shift Scheduling Specification

## 1. Staffing Ratio Standard
- **Banquet Captain**: 1 captain per event (lead coordinator).
- **Buffet Waiters**: 1 waiter per 25-30 guests.
- **Beverage Server**: 1 server per 50 guests.
- **Kitchen Stewards & Dishwashers**: 1 steward per 75 guests.

## 2. Scheduling Constraints
System validates staff availability against existing shift assignments, enforcing mandatory rest intervals of at least 8 hours between consecutive catering events.
