# Banquet Service Standard Milestone Timeline Guide

## 1. Standard Timeline Stages
- **T - 4 Hours**: Kitchen food preparation and dispatch loading.
- **T - 3 Hours**: Ingress arrival at venue; table arrangement and linen draping.
- **T - 1 Hour**: Food warming and buffet table garnishing.
- **T - 0**: Buffet opens; guest dining service begins.
- **T + 2.5 Hours**: Buffet closes; leftover food packaging for client.
- **T + 3.5 Hours**: Egress cleanup, chinaware packing, and venue turnover.
