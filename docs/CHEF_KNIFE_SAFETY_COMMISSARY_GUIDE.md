# Commercial Kitchen Knife Safety & Sanitation Standards

## 1. Knife Handling Rules
- **Color-Coded Handles**: Red for raw beef/pork, Yellow for raw poultry, Blue for seafood, Green for vegetables, White for cooked foods.
- **Sanitation**: Submerged in food-grade sanitizer solution ($200\text{ ppm}$ quaternary ammonium) between tasks.
- **Sharpening**: Professional whetstone honing (1000/6000 grit) performed weekly to ensure clean cuts and operator safety.
