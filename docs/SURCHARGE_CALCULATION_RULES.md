# Surcharge & Ancillary Fee Calculation Rules

## 1. Surcharge Categories
- **Out-of-Town Delivery**: Calculated based on kilometer distance from central commissary (standard ₱50/km beyond 15km).
- **Stairway Hauling Fee**: Applied to venues without service elevators above the ground floor (₱500 per flight of stairs).
- **Overtime Waiting Fee**: ₱1,500/hour if event dining exceeds contracted service hours.
