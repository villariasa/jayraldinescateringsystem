# Commissary Cold Chain Logistics & HACCP Temperature Rules

## 1. Temperature Thresholds
- **Walk-in Chillers**: 1°C to 4°C (34°F to 39°F).
- **Deep Freezers**: -18°C or lower (0°F).
- **Hot Holding Cambros**: Minimum 60°C (140°F) during transport.
- **Cold Holding Cambros**: Maximum 5°C (41°F) during transport.

## 2. Log Verification
Kitchen stewards must record calibrated probe thermometer readings onto the digital dispatch log prior to loading transport vans.
