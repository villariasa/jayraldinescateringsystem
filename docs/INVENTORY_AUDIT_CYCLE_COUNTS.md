# Monthly Commissary Inventory Cycle Counts & Auditing Policy

## 1. Cycle Count Frequency
- **High-Value Proteins (Beef, Seafood)**: Weekly physical weight audit.
- **Dry Goods & Seasonings**: Bi-weekly box count.
- **Beverages & Packaging**: Monthly unit count.

## 2. Variance Tolerance
Variances under 1.5% are categorized as normal cooking shrinkage. Any variance exceeding 3% triggers an automatic managerial investigation.
