# Banquet Program Flow & Audio-Visual Synchronization Guide

## 1. Program Milestones
- **Guest Arrival & Registration**: Soft ambient acoustic music.
- **Grand Entrance**: Uplighting transition, fanfares, and emcee introduction.
- **Ceremonial Cake Cutting**: Champagne pouring and spotlighting.
- **Buffet Blessing & Ribbon Cutting**: Emcee announces buffet opening by table clusters.
