# Catering Fleet Preventive Maintenance Schedule

## 1. Maintenance Intervals
- **Every 5,000 km**: Engine oil replacement, oil filter change, tire pressure & brake pad inspection.
- **Every 20,000 km**: Fuel filter replacement, transmission fluid check, air conditioning system sanitation.
- **Pre-Trip Checklist**: Daily coolant check, tire tread inspection, and emergency jack verification prior to event dispatch.
