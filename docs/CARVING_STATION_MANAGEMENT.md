# Live Roast Carving Station Operation Specification

## 1. Station Layout
- Granite cutting board with liquid drip groove.
- Heat lamp bridge maintaining surface warmth.
- Dual carving knives (grantee slicing knife and carving fork).

## 2. Portioning Standards
- **Roast Beef Tenderloin**: Sliced at a 45-degree angle across the grain; 80g to 100g per guest.
- **Lechon Belly Roll**: Crisp crackling cut with shears; succulent meat portioned with cleaver.
