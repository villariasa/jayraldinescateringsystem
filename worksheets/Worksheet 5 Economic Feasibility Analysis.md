# Worksheet No. 5: Economic Feasibility Analysis

### 1. Economic Feasibility Executive Summary
A comprehensive cost–benefit analysis was performed for the **Jayraldine's Catering Management System** by the systems analysis team (Mikka Armenta, Daniel Grant Ilustrisimo, Rey Anderson Navarro, and Medy Villarias). Conservative financial forecasts show that transitioning from the manual, paper-based clear book workflow to the proposed digital catering management platform will substantially improve the business's operating margins, revenue retention, and financial performance over a three-year planning horizon.

* **Return on Investment (ROI):** **234.63%** over three (3) operating years, indicating that every peso invested in the system yields approximately ₱2.35 in net financial returns.
* **Net Present Value (NPV):** **₱521,968** (evaluated at a 10% required rate of return / discount rate), confirming robust financial viability well above the benchmark threshold.
* **Break-Even Point (BEP):** **0.58 years** (~7 months into operational Year 1), demonstrating rapid capital recovery through immediate cost savings and eliminated billing leakages.
* **Total Cumulative Net Cash Flow:** **₱664,000** generated over the three-year lifecycle (₱947,000 Total Tangible Benefits versus ₱283,000 Total Development and Operational Costs).

### 4. Project Economic Value Calculations

#### 4.1 Return on Investment (ROI)
The Return on Investment measures the percentage return earned on the total funds invested in the project:
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{ROI} = \frac{\text{Total Benefits} - \text{Total Costs}}{\text{Total Costs}}$$
* **Calculation:**  
  $$\text{ROI} = \frac{₱947,000 - ₱283,000}{₱283,000} = \frac{₱664,000}{₱283,000} = \mathbf{234.63\%}$$
* **Interpretation:** An ROI of 234.63% indicates an outstanding financial return, where net earnings over three years exceed total development and ongoing operating costs by more than 2.3 times.

#### 4.2 Break-Even Point (BEP / Payback Period)
The break-even point calculates the time required to recover the initial project investment from operational net cash flows. Cumulative net cash flow turns positive during Year 1 (2027):
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{BEP} = \text{Years of Negative Cash Flow} + \frac{\text{Year 1 Net Cash Flow} - \text{Year 1 Cumulative Cash Flow}}{\text{Year 1 Net Cash Flow}}$$
* **Calculation:**  
  $$\text{BEP} = 0 + \frac{₱232,500 - ₱97,500}{₱232,500} = \frac{₱135,000}{₱232,500} = \mathbf{0.58\text{ years}}\text{ (~6.96 months)}$$
* **Interpretation:** The initial investment of ₱135,000 is fully recovered in under seven (7) months of live operation, representing minimal financial exposure for the business.

#### 4.3 Net Present Value (NPV)
Net Present Value evaluates project profitability in today's currency terms by applying a 10% annual discount rate to future cash flows:
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{NPV} = \sum \text{PV of Total Benefits} - \sum \text{PV of Total Costs}$$
* **PV of Benefits:** ₱0 (Yr 0) + ₱254,545 (Yr 1) + ₱260,331 (Yr 2) + ₱264,463 (Yr 3) = **₱779,339**
* **PV of Costs:** ₱135,000 (Yr 0) + ₱43,182 (Yr 1) + ₱40,496 (Yr 2) + ₱38,693 (Yr 3) = **₱257,370**
* **Calculation:**  
  $$\text{NPV} = ₱779,339 - ₱257,370 = \mathbf{₱521,968}$$
* **Decision Rule:** Because NPV (₱521,968) is significantly greater than zero (NPV > 0), the project is economically acceptable and highly lucrative.
